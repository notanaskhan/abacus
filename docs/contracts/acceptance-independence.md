## 1. Purpose
Consumers can rely on: ENG-05's team roster extras (budget hours, the portal flag, the EP's team approval and its "changes since" facts, the EQR candidate list with eligibility per check, EQR assignment and the quality owner's eligibility approval); CLI-07's acceptance record (questionnaire sections from the firm's pinned template version, flags with sources, manual checks, send to partner, risk-partner review, the three-option decision with conditions); CLI-08's independence summary (confirmations, banner reasons, conclusion), rotation, NAS rows, breaches, reminders and the EP's conclusion; CLI-06's conflict checks with redacted walled matches, clearance and human-recorded screening.

**Behaviour.** Every route first refuses a client principal, an agent and the system and authorises its action (a walled or unknown engagement 404), and only then reads its flag (DEC-326). With `engagements.acceptance_v2` off, every acceptance, independence (except the reminders), conflict and screening route answers 404; with it on, the acceptance (P-07-T35), conflict and screening (P-07-T36) and independence (P-07-T41) routes are live. An engagement with no acceptance record answers 404 on the acceptance routes; a record is opened by ENG-03's "Start acceptance" (`engagements-v2.md`) or, later, the intake commit (P-07-T42).

**Acceptance as built (P-07-T35, DEC-372).** The record is pinned to the newest version of the firm's questionnaire template for the engagement type's family (database content; none: 409 `acceptance_template_missing` on start). A section's `version` is 0 until its first answer, and `seen_version` on an answer is the section's. A manual check is asked of someone who holds the prepare right on this engagement (engagement partner, manager, client service; 422 `invalid` otherwise, or while the section has an open check) and only that person completes it (403). Client service may do nothing else here. Send to partner needs `seen_version` (the record's) and no open check; after it the answers, the risk rating and manual checks are frozen (409 `acceptance_sent`; DEC-375), while flags and the risk-partner review continue. The decision needs an Awaiting-partner record (409 `acceptance_not_ready` in Draft), the record's `seen_version`, a rationale for every option (SPEC-332 AC-7 and the CLI-07 deviation), conditions only with Accept with conditions (422 `invalid` otherwise; each assignee an active firm member), and on a record ever rated High (`high_risk_seen`, even if lowered in Draft) a completed risk-partner review. The decider must be a partner-level member at decision time, and the engagement partner named on a draft must be partner-level to start acceptance (422 `invalid`, `facts.field` `team`; DEC-375). The risk partner is a partner-level member who is neither the caller nor an engagement partner here nor walled from the client (422 `invalid`), and only they complete the review, provided they hold no engagement partner seat here by then (403); the partner who completed the review never decides the record (403; DEC-375). A decided or declined record is locked at the database, and the database also requires (0165) an Awaiting-partner record, the acting person as `decided_by`, that person the engagement's engagement partner at partner level, and a completed review on a record ever rated High; G1 counts a decision only on a record moved to decided or declined. SPEC-025's `PUT …/acceptance` stays as an adapter: on an engagement with a record it refuses any decision other than the record's (409 `decision_locked`).

**Independence as built (P-07-T41, DEC-428).** The members are the team holding a role asked to confirm on joining (engagement partner, manager, senior, staff, reviewer, EQR reviewer, specialist); `confirmed` counts their confirmations. A confirmation is the caller's own (there is no user id in the body; someone with no such role is refused at the matrix): exactly three distinct `statement_codes` (422 `invalid` otherwise) of the caller's `statement_version` (the statement words are data), stored with the codes; an optional interests note and a decline's note are sealed per tenant, and a decline's note is read only by the person and by whoever holds `independence.conclude` here. `annual_confirmed` is the member's annual confirmation for the firm's current year read from G-01 (`has_current_annual_confirmation`), given only to a reader who holds `independence.annual_read` (null otherwise); it does not yet join the cleared predicate (DEC-428). The banner's `blockers`: `member_not_confirmed` and `member_declined` (`user`), `conflict_not_cleared` (`conflict_result`: a result still pending, in counterparty review or a conflict on a check run for the client or one of its entities, matches on the client itself left out), and on an attest client `ske_designee_missing` (`engagement`) and `nas_not_evaluated` (`nas_evaluation`, or `engagement` for a service with no row yet), and `breach_open` (`independence_breach`). The conclusion needs every blocker but `breach_open` gone (409 `independence_preconditions_unmet` with one count per reason code as facts), a partner-level engagement partner (the database checks the same) and fresh MFA; it supersedes the newest (one successor each). NAS rows (attest clients only; a non-attest client answers no rows and a PUT is 422 `invalid`, `facts.field` `attest_client`) are the client's engagements outside the attest family the firm provides (accepted to delivered), one per engagement type, plus any row written; a PUT needs an SKE designee (409 `independence_preconditions_unmet`, `ske_designee_missing`), and a status other than `pending` needs `independence.conclude` here (403). A breach event kind is recorded once (409 `illegal_transition`); a breach is resolved when all three are. G2 is met on the newest conclusion (Independent, or with safeguards) while no breach is open, and reads SPEC-025's conclusion where the engagement has no conclusion row (blockers `independence_conclusion`, `independence_breach`). Security review (DEC-429): SPEC-025's `PUT …/acceptance` refuses `independence_concluded` with 409 `decision_locked` once `engagements.acceptance_v2` is on or the engagement has a CLI-07 record, and a conclusion it writes after 0175 never meets G2 (only `engagement_acceptance` rows from before 0175, `legacy_g2`, count); the database binds every independence answer to the acting person (`app.actor_id`, `app.actor_kind = human`); a conclusion supersedes only one of its own engagement; a NAS service code must be one the firm provides the client outside the attest family (422 `invalid`, `facts.field` `service_code`), and after the engagement partner decided a row only they record another evaluation (409 `decision_locked`); a standing conflict hit on a client the reader is walled from appears in the banner as one `conflict_not_cleared` naming the engagement, with no result id; G2's "met at" is the conclusion's time. Rotation counts the engagements back along the `prior_engagement_id` chain on whose team the person sat without a gap; `review_years` is 7 until firm settings hold integers. Reminders count on the member's row (`reminder_count`).

**Conflicts as built (P-07-T36, DEC-373).** A check searches, per name and as a system principal over metadata (2-second statement timeout): clients and client entities by normalised name (`exact`, or `trigram` at a `pg_trgm` similarity of at least 0.6), contacts the same unless the contact holds a counterparty role (custodian, recordkeeper, TPA, payroll provider), which is `counterparty` on its client, and each matched entity's current relationship edges to another client's entity (`relationship`). One result per matched client and kind, the best score. A counterparty result starts as `counterparty_review`, any other as `pending`; the newest clearance event is the assessment, and the check's `outcome` is `hits` while any result is `pending`, `counterparty_review` or `conflict`. Results are redacted per reader at read time: every match on a client the reader is walled from collapses into one row (`redacted: true`, the first such result's id, `match_kind` `trigram` whatever the real kind, `assessment` `pending`) and the check's `restricted_match: true`; the `outcome` is `hits` when a visible match stands or `restricted_match` is true, never `clear` beside a restricted match (DEC-375). The whole search has one 2-second budget across its three statements (503 past it). A draft subject must be one its owner module says the caller may see (an engagement draft: the caller's own; an intake draft: refused until P-07-T42 registers it), and a screening record's evidence link a file the caller may read (404 otherwise); a client or client-entity subject the caller cannot see answers 404 on run and read; draft subjects are references organisations does not read. Names are never stored on results (read at display time); the searched names, reasons and notes are sealed. With `engagements.v2` off, ENG-05's team rows and the reminders answer 404; with it on they are live (P-07-T30; DEC-327 has the eligibility rules).

## 3. Models
Code: `backend/src/abacus/modules/engagements/acceptance_contract.py` and `backend/src/abacus/modules/organisations/conflicts_contract.py`. Every field carries a classification tag; free text is confidential and sealed at rest.

- **Acceptance:** `AcceptanceRecordOut` (`kind` new_client · new_engagement_existing_client · continuance; `status` C2 `acceptance_record`; `template_version_id` and `template_name` from the firm's database template; `risk_rating` low · moderate · high; `sections` `AcceptanceSectionOut` with `answer_state` pending · complete · flag · manual_check_open · manual_check_done and `manual_check`; `flags` `AcceptanceFlagOut` with `severity` low · medium · high and `sources` (`kind` evidence_version · request_item); `decision` `AcceptanceDecisionOut` with `option` accept · accept_with_conditions · decline; `conditions`; `risk_partner_review`; `prior` for a continuance; `version`; `allowed_actions`). Inputs: `AcceptanceAnswersIn` (`seen_version`, `answers`, `complete`, `risk_rating`), `AcceptanceFlagIn`, `ManualCheckRequestIn`, `ManualCheckCompleteIn`, `SendToPartnerIn`, `ConditionCompleteIn`, `RiskPartnerReviewIn`, `RiskPartnerReviewCompleteIn` (empty), `AcceptanceDecisionIn` (`option`, `rationale`, `conditions` with assignee and optional due date, `seen_version`).
- **Independence:** `IndependenceSummaryOut` (`attest_client`, `confirmed` of `total`, `members` `MemberConfirmationOut` with `confirmation` requested · confirmed · declined and `annual_confirmed` (P-07-T41), `blockers` `IndependenceBlockerOut` reason codes, `conclusion` `IndependenceConclusionOut` with `option` C2 `independence_conclusion` and `supersedes_id`, `ske_designee_contact_id`), `RotationOut` (`review_years`, rows with `years_served` and `familiarity_flag`), `ConfirmIn` (`statement_version`, `statement_codes`, `interests_note`), `DeclineIn` (`note`), `ConfirmationOut`, `RemindersIn` / `RemindersOut`, `NasOut` / `NasRowOut` (`status` C2 `nas_evaluation`) / `NasEvaluationIn`, `BreachIn`, `BreachEventIn` (`kind` evaluated · tcwg_communicated · remediated), `BreachOut` (`resolved`), `ConcludeIn` (`option`, `safeguards`).
- **Team and roles:** `TeamApproveIn` (`member_set_hash`), `TeamApprovalOut` (`member_set_hash` of the current team, `approved_by`, `approved_by_name`, `approved_at`, `changed`, `added_since`), `PortalVisibilityIn` / `Out`, `EqrCandidateOut` (`eligibility` eligible · not_eligible, `checks` `EqrCheckOut` with `code` not_on_team · cooling_off · competence · independence · capacity, `result` pass · fail · pending · not_assessed, `count` for competence, `year` for cooling-off, `current`), `EqrIn` / `EqrOut`, `EqrEligibilityApprovalIn` / `Out`. The live team routes (`GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /team`) take `specialist` and `budget_hours` (PUT: role and hours, either or both) and return `TeamRosterMemberOut` (`TeamMemberOut` + `budget_hours`, `shown_on_portal`, `proposed_at`, `independence` requested · confirmed · declined); C1's engagement projection keeps `TeamMemberOut` (P-07-T30, DEC-327).
- **Conflicts:** `ConflictCheckIn` (`subject` kind and id, `names` 1–50), `ConflictCheckOut` (`outcome` pending · clear · hits over the matches the reader may see, `results`, `restricted_match`), `ConflictResultOut` (`redacted`, `match_kind` exact · trigram · relationship · counterparty, `assessment` C2 `conflict_assessment`; when `redacted` is true, `matched_client_id`, `matched_name`, `matched_ref_*` and `score` are null), `ConflictClearIn` (`assessment` clear · same_family_not_adverse · not_adverse · counterparty_review · conflict, `reason`), `ScreeningRecordIn` / `Out` (`screening_list` ofac_sdn · adverse_media · restricted_entities, `result` no_match · match · not_applicable, `evidence_version_id`).

No model has a hidden-by-wall field or a count of walled matches (DEC-43, DEC-64).

## 4. Status words
C2: `acceptance_record`, `nas_evaluation`, `independence_conclusion`, `conflict_assessment`. The clearance code `conflict` (AC-25) joined `conflict_assessment` with P-07-T36 ("Conflict", red-tint, terminal). Confirmation states (requested, confirmed, declined), answer states and EQR results are fields not named `status`, so they are not registry statuses.

## 5. Human-only actions
`acceptance.decide`, `acceptance.complete_review`, `independence.confirm`, `independence.conclude`, `conflict.clear`, `team.approve` and `eqr.approve_eligibility` deny `agent` and `system` at the matrix (the generated matrix and resolution tests hold it from the stub onward), at `require_live_human` in the live services, and at an `actor_kind = 'human'` CHECK on the rows they write. Their audit actions (`acceptance.decided`, `independence.confirmed`, `independence.concluded`, `conflict.cleared`, `team.approved`, `eqr.eligibility_approved`) join `docs/architecture/human-only-actions.yaml` with the migration that first writes them (`team.approved` and `eqr.eligibility_approved` with 0155; `acceptance.decided` and `acceptance.review_completed` with 0163; `conflict.cleared` with 0164; `independence.concluded` and `nas.evaluated` with 0175). Only `engagements/acceptance.py`, its route and `engagements/api.py` name `decide_acceptance` (banned pattern ACCEPT-001); only `organisations/conflicts.py`, its route and `organisations/api.py` name `clear_conflict_result` (CONFLICT-001); only `engagements/independence.py`, its route and `engagements/api.py` name `conclude_independence` (INDEP-001).
