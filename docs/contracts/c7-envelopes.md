## 1. Purpose
One envelope model for every signed document, whichever provider: `record` (imported, already signed), `docuseal` (provider signing, ADR-342; `docusign` stays readable on drafts made before it) or `fake` (synthetic tenants). Sources (engagement letters, consents, e-file authorizations, rep letters, notices) register with `signatures.api.register_envelope_source` and get their gate facts back.

## 7. Module API
`signatures.api.register_envelope_source(kind, resolver)`, `envelope_summary_for(ctx, source_kind, source_id)`, `envelope_gate_facts(ctx, gate_kind, target_ref)` (G3, e-file authorization), `file_signed_copy` through `evidence.api.file_to_folder` (Signed authorizations).

**v1.1 (additive, N-02-T25, DEC-1285; for T-01 and T-02):** the `consent_set` (purpose `consent_7216`, family `consent`) and `tax_return` (`form_8879`, `form_8879_pe`, `form_8879_f`; family `efile_authorization`) source contracts: such a source must provide `draft`, and its drafts stay inside those purposes (422 `source_contract_broken`). `supersede_for_amount_change(tenant, envelope_id, binding_id)`: the system only (`SystemOnly` otherwise), a permanent marker "Amounts changed after signing · re-sign required" (0432's trigger refuses a person, an agent and any clearing); a superseded envelope keeps its evidence and status and is unmet for every gate; emits `envelope.superseded`. `extend_retention(tenant, envelope_id, until)`: forward only; locks every stored record of the envelope until at least `until` and moves `envelopes.retain_until`; an earlier date is 409 `retention_not_extended`. `envelope_summaries_for(ctx, work_item_ids)`: per work item, its envelopes with status, counts, `superseded` and signer chips (first name, role, status), no documents. `envelope_gate_facts(ctx, 'efile_authorization', tax_return_id)`: met by a completed, non-superseded `tax_return` envelope. `EnvelopeSummary` gains `superseded`. Signer-level events `envelope.signer_signed` and `envelope.signer_verification_failed` (identifiers, the source kind and id) from the poll.

## N-02-T33 additions (DEC-1468)
Request approval, approve, send and amend are live behind `engagements.letter_composer` (`engagements/letter_flow.py`; send also needs `signatures.envelopes`). `POST …/letter/request-approval` (`letter.draft`, a person, `If-Match` the version) stamps the draft (`approval_requested_at`, `approval_requested_by`, 0563), emits `letter.approval_requested` (C16 `letter_approval_requested` to each engagement partner) and opens C13 `letter_partner_approval` (kind `decision`, one gate per partner, `assigned_only`). `POST …/letter/approve` (`letter.approve`: engagement partner, fresh second factor, independence; `If-Match`) needs G1 decided on or before the letter's date (409 `letter_before_acceptance`; G1 undecided: 409 `gate_unmet`), stores the approver, time and the draft's content hash, and renders the draft final through C11 with approval kind `letter_partner_approval` (`final_document_version_id`); audit `letter.approved` is human-only (63 entries), and the database refuses `partner_approved` from any transaction but the approver's own. An edit after approval is a new Draft version; the approval stays, stale, on the superseded one. `POST …/letter/send` (`letter.send`, a person) drafts an envelope from the `engagement_letter` source, whose `source_id` is the approved letter version: purpose and policy family `engagement_letter`, the C11 final (sha256 = the approved content hash until P-11-T11 writes bytes), the client's signing officer (`email_code`, group 1) then the approving partner (`firm_sso`, `countersigner`, group 2). The version keeps the envelope's id; a second Send answers it; the letter takes no edit after it (409 `letter_locked`); the source's pre-send check answers `letter_changed` when the version is no longer the current approved letter. `envelope.send_approved` moves the letter to Sent and `envelope.completed` to Signed, locking its C11 document. `POST …/letter/amend` (`letter.amend`, 201) on a signed letter inserts an `addendum` draft amending it, with today's facts, rendered as its own C11 document. Errors: 409 `letter_not_approvable`, `letter_not_sendable`, `letter_not_amendable`, `letter_signer_missing` (no signing officer designated). Audit: `letter.approval_requested`, `letter.approved`, `letter.envelope_drafted`, `letter.sent`, `letter.signed`, `letter.amended`, `letter.envelope_ignored`. §7216 consents travel in their own `consent_set` envelope; ENG-04's consents rows and the approval dialog are N-02-T35's.
## N-02-T27 additions (DEC-1469)
`GET /v1/envelope-policies` is live: the four families, each the firm's row or the published floor (`version` 0 until a firm saves one). The floor: e-file authorization `kba` required (allowed `kba`, `in_person_id`, `firm_sso`; SMS has no value), `kba_max_attempts` 3, `failure_path` `wet_signature`; letters, consents and general `email_code` (letters require a countersignature). `PUT /v1/envelope-policies/{family}` (`envelope_policy.manage`: security admin, fresh second factor) is stricter-only against the floor: 409 `policy_not_stricter` (methods only narrow, the e-file family keeps `kba` as default, the attempt limit only falls, a required countersignature stays), 409 `stale_version`. The provider poll counts each new identity-check failure (`signer.kba_failed`, one event each) and at the family's limit sets the signer `verification_failed` whatever the provider reports, abandons its open signing sessions and emits `envelope.signer_verification_failed` (additive `sent_by`; C16 `envelope_signer_verification_failed` to the sender); the poll never moves a signed, declined or closed signer again. `POST …/signers/{sid}/wet-signature` is live (`envelope.record_wet_signature`, a person; `Idempotency-Key`): the scan is a file staged through `/import/files`; additive optional `received_on` and `in_person_id {id_type, checked_by_user_id}` (the checker must be the recorder); allowed for a `verification_failed` signer or an `in_person_id` signer not yet signed, on an open envelope whose earlier routing groups have signed; else 409 `wet_signature_not_allowed`. The signer becomes signed with `signature_method` `handwritten` and `wet_recorded_by` (0564; the database refuses any other recorder), the scan and a manifest go write-once to the records bucket, the scan is filed as the signed copy, and when every signer has signed the envelope completes; the completion activity records the remote signers' evidence and emits `envelope.completed` (skipping the provider when all signed by hand). `SignerChip` adds `signer_id` and `locked`. The e-file family's send checks add `ero_before_taxpayer` under `ero_present` (Pub 1345).

## N-02-T38 additions (DEC-1567)
The evidence package: a §7216 consent's package is kept under the retention record type `consent_evidence` (signature plus three years at least), every other envelope's under `signature_evidence` (never below the firm's floor); a type with no policy falls back to `signature_evidence`. `extend_retention` moves an e-file authorization to the IRS received date plus three years when T-01 imports the accepted acknowledgement. Each package carries a legible reproduction page (per signer: date and time, masked IP and login, identity result, method, the signed image's and documents' SHA-256), drawn by C11's renderer; a renderer failure never blocks completion and records `envelope.legible_copy_failed`. A nightly job re-hashes every stored record an envelope's evidence names; a missing or changed one records `envelope.integrity_failed` once and raises `signatures_evidence_integrity_failures`.
