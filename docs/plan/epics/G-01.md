# G-01 · Firm settings, users, roles, offices & walls

> Epic specification and task backlog. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for dates, owners and scope. Decision and ADR IDs are chapter 09's (DEC-nn, ADR P-nn); where chapters 03 and 06 minted different P-numbers, chapter 09's IDs and titles are used. **N** = notanaskhan, **AY** = ayushkbhatia. The CSV twin of §9 is `sections/epics/G-01.tasks.csv`.
>
> **Short version.** G-01 turns Firm settings into the Accrued T12 settings frame and makes the firm's people model real. It covers three screens:
> 1. **ADM-01 Settings home & firm profile** (P0): the 14-section settings frame every ADM screen reuses, the firm profile, offices summary, plan and seats, firm-wide defaults, and an "At a glance" card fed by a registration slot that later epics plug into.
> 2. **ADM-02 Users & roles** (P0): the 46 seat holders, invitations against the seat limit, the person drawer, role changes and deactivation, seasonal access end dates, the read-only capability grid generated from the permission matrix (the Accrued Agent column is locked and can never approve), and the annual independence confirmation that DEC-91 places in this screen.
> 3. **ADM-03 Offices, teams, walls & offshore** (P1): offices as first-class filters (DEC-30), teams and review groups, service-line content separation (revision R2), **walls v2** (reason, named partner approver, relationship-partner conflict, 404 semantics with no hidden counts) and the §7216 **consent-gated offshore group**, which fails closed until T-01 registers the consent provider.
>
> G-01 provides no numbered contract in the chapter 01 register. It ships one family contract, `docs/contracts/firm-settings.md`, under the §1.3.2 handshake, and it consumes C1, C2, C13 and C16.

### 3.1 Settings frame and firm profile (ADM-01)

**Story.** As the firm admin, I want the firm's identity, region, offices, plan and defaults in one calm form, with a one-line health status for every settings area, so that I can see in seconds what needs attention and jump there.

- **AC1.1 (ADM-01a).** *Given* Grace at E0, *when* she opens `/settings/general`, *then* the toolbar has no active nav item and the firm pill is pressed; the subbar reads "‹ Back · Firm settings · Brightwater Hale LLP"; the section list shows the built sections in their group order with General selected; Save is disabled; offices read Chicago · HQ 22 seats, Denver 14, New York 10, total 46; Plan & seats reads "46 of 46 used" with the amber helper, People "52 · 7 partners · 37 professional staff · 8 operations (6 back-office staff hold no seat)", renewal Jan 1, 2027.
- **AC1.2 (ADM-01b, f).** *Given* a clean form, *when* Grace picks Eastern Time, *then* the header shows "Unsaved changes" and Save enables; *when* she saves, *then* the client sends `PATCH /v1/firm/settings/profile` with the `version` it read; a stale MFA opens the T12 step-up slot (`ConfirmItsYou`); on success the toast "Firm settings saved" appears, the form is clean again, and one `audit_events` row `firm_setting.updated` names Grace (INV-5) with the key, never the value of a Confidential field.
- **AC1.3 (ADM-01c).** *Given* Display name is emptied, *then* the value area reads "Required" with the helper "Enter the name clients see in the portal.", Save is blocked client-side, and the server also answers `422 {detail:"display_name_required"}` if called.
- **AC1.4 (concurrency).** *Given* Grace and Felix load the form, *when* Grace saves and Felix (if he had write rights) saves the same section with the old `version`, *then* the second answers `409 stale_version` and the UI shows "Changed by Grace Holloway — reload".
- **AC1.5 (region).** *Then* Primary region is never editable: no route accepts it, and `firms.region` changes only through provisioning (staff issuer).
- **AC1.6 (ADM-01d).** *Given* Daniel (level partner), *then* every value renders in ink-2 with a lock on each band, the amber banner reads "Only firm admins can edit these settings.", there is no Save, and At a glance is visible; `PATCH` answers 403 `role`.
