## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Rebuild the relationship spine of Accrued on the new model. Every client has one record, every engagement is a contract with a server-enforced lifecycle, and gates G1 (acceptance), G2 (independence) and G3 (contract) are performed in the product by named humans and enforced on the server. This epic delivers the client index and record, contacts and scoped portal access, single and bulk client intake for pilot onboarding, acceptance and continuance, independence and conflicts, the data-driven engagement wizard, team and roles, and a roll-forward re-skin. No agent decides anything here, and no model is called (§6).
