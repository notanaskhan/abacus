## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Give an audit team at a pilot firm one place to see its assurance portfolio, open an engagement into a cockpit that every later audit epic plugs into, and run the PBC list beside the documents themselves — while the firm's own workpaper tool stays authoritative. The cockpit is built on the work-item model (`/audits/$auditId` is the assurance work item), its rail is registration-driven so A-02…A-11 add sections without editing A-01, and the evidence side reuses P-06's requests engine and P-03's viewer. The **binder export** is the ADR-036 open package (evidence, index, provenance, audit trail, SHA-256 manifest) delivered after fresh MFA through a short-lived, authenticated download, so any pilot can roll back on the day and Dec-YE reports can be issued from the firm's tool until A-07. The agent screens and proposes; named people accept, return, record and export (INV-1).

**Story 6.3.** Who may export, and what the agent can never do.
- *Given* Ana (staff), Grace (firm admin), `client_service`, an offshore member, a walled member, a client principal, an agent, system or support context, *then* create answers 403 by role (staff, firm admin, `client_service`), 403 `offshore`, 404 (walled; INV-3), 403 (client; INV-4) or 403 with the DB refusing a non-human `requested_by` (agent, system, support; INV-1); download follows the same rules plus "requester or an `export.create` holder on that engagement".
- *Given* a `tax_return` work item, *then* 422 `export_kind_unsupported` (fail closed until C19 and a tax-export review).

**Story 6.4.** Exports expire and never outlive their content.
- *Given* a ready export, *when* 7 days pass on `kernel.clock`, *then* status `expired`, download answers 410 `export_expired`, and the exports bucket lifecycle removes the segments.
- *Given* a ready export that included an unaccepted upload the client later withdraws, *then* the export is voided (`expired`, reason `withdrawn_content`) and its segments are deleted.
- *Given* a stored evidence object whose decrypted SHA-256 does not match its fingerprint, *then* the build fails with `integrity_mismatch`, nothing is published, and the RB-29 integrity alarm fires.

### 3.7 Cross-cutting invariants

- **INV-1:** the agent principal holds none of `evidence.accept`, `evidence.reject`, `fulfilment.confirm`, `assurance.record_phase`, `assurance.record_release`, `assurance.record_archive`, `export.create`, `export.download`; these rows are added to chapter 06's I-AGENT-NEVER list (`test_agent_never.py`, Q-02) by T25.
- **INV-2:** the AUD-03 viewer lights only regions from verified C4 citations; the export copies citation rows, never unverified quotes.

- **INV-4:** no `/v1/audits*`, `/v1/work-items/{id}/exports` or `/v1/exports/*` route is reachable by a client principal; draft requests stay firm-only (P-06).
- **INV-5:** every write names its person; agent proposals read "‹person› via Agent" where a person initiated them; the export manifest records `requested_by` and `downloaded_by`.
