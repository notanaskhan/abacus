- **AICPA SSTS on knowledge of error and amended returns:** the firm documents the error and the client's decision; Accrued keeps the original immutable and the amendment's citations (verify the 2024 SSTS section numbering with the tax CPA).
- **ADR-111 / ADR P-25:** model-drafted text reaches a client only under every P-25 condition and never on a real firm in T-08's window.
- **Retention and holds (P-14, DEC-71):** retention rows for manifests, approvals, explanations, runs and results, planning opportunities (TRI-derived), amendments, supersessions and AAR elections × service line `tax` are requested from Q-05 as database content; holds apply; T-08 destroys nothing.
- **Walls and need-to-know (ADR-024, ADR-026, DEC-43):** 404 semantics and no hidden counts; firm admins see deadline metadata only.
- **Hostile content (ADR-052):** receipts, 1099s, invoices and client messages are data, never instructions.
- **SOC 2:** every state change is audited; exports need fresh MFA and are logged.
