## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Give every audit engagement one trial balance of record per label, built from an immutable ledger snapshot (file import or, from S14, the first live ledger read), shown on AUD-06 with code-computed layers, ties and leadsheets and line-level provenance; give clients the POR-16 page where they, and only they, connect, reconnect or disconnect their systems, always beside "Export instead"; and stand up the `integrations` framework and the write-capability registry so that later epics add adapters without ever creating an undeclared write.

**When A-02 ships (S17, behind flags; per-firm flips from M9/M10), at Brightwater Hale:**

- **Simone Baptiste** (Senior, Granite Peak) uploads Granite Peak's FY2026 NetSuite TB export (the existing SPEC-028 import), or pulls it from a connected ledger, and clicks *Use this trial balance* on AUD-06. The TB appears as **Unadjusted · TB: Standard**, balanced, with total assets **26,410,000.00**, FY2025 in the Beginning of Year column and leadsheets by audit area. Caption: "Imported by Simone Baptiste from a NetSuite export, Sep 11, 10:02 AM".
- **Marcus Delaney** (Senior, Northwind) loads Northwind's PY2025 plan TB from the Accrued TB template, keyed from the Harborline trust report, agrees each line to the report through the viewer (a ✓ that cites "Harborline Net Trust Assets p.1, line 7"), and defines the three ties. The check strip reads **tie · tie · tie**, or **off 1,560.00** when BrokerWindow does not agree (AUD-06c), with the line in red ink and a flag that links to an issue.
- **Hannah Leclerc** (Senior Manager) opens the same TB, sees "✓ HL" on the lines she reviewed and plain ✓ on Marcus's agreements, clicks a chip to read who signed what, switches to **Leadsheets** for the investments rollup (PY · CY · Δ · Δ%), downloads it with **Open in Excel**, and, once P-04's SHL-12 ships (S19), signs it off at L2. A re-import becomes **v2**; her earlier sign-offs show "Changed after sign-off" (P-04), never edited.
- **Tess Moreno** (Owner, Copperleaf) opens **Connections** in the portal, reads exactly what Brightwater Hale will and won't do, turns on the authorization, signs in on QuickBooks' own site and comes back **Connected**. She can see details and the last five syncs, retry a failed sync, reconnect when a feed needs it, or disconnect, knowing that what Brightwater Hale already received stays in her client file. Beside every ledger she sees **Export instead**.
- **Hannah** asks a client to reconnect from AUD-06's source menu; the client gets one "Reconnect request" email in Brightwater Hale's name and a to-do on POR-02 that clears when they reconnect.
- Nobody, and no agent, can write into a client's system: the registry holds no enabled write, every write call is refused and audited, and a W1-shaped entry is refused for attest clients.

## 3. Features & user stories

### 3.1 Adopt a trial balance of record (AUD-06 empty → a)

*As Simone Baptiste, I want to make an imported or pulled TB the engagement's Unadjusted TB, so that every workpaper reads one immutable source.*

- **AC-1.1** *Given* Granite Peak's FY2026 audit with no TB, *when* Simone opens `/audits/$auditId/trial-balance`, *then* AUD-06 shows the empty state with *Import trial balance…* (her `ledger_import.upload`), *Use connected ledger* (only when the entity has a live ledger connection) and *Ask client to connect or export*, and no figures.
