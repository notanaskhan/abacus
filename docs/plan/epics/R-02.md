## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Give management and those charged with governance one white-labelled place to **decide** and to **receive**: (1) **portal approvals (C18)** — a registry of client decisions that any module can put to a named client person (accept or decline proposed audit entries, acknowledge the uncorrected ones, approve the financial-statement draft, approve a monthly package), each decided only by a live, MFA-verified client human holding the right grant and designation, bound to the exact version they saw, and handed back to the owning module that records it; (2) a **deliverables and release registry with download audit (C10)** — every document the firm delivers, by work item, watermarked while draft and final after release, with every page view and download recorded; (3) a **governance room** for AU-C 260/265/703 communications, per-member acknowledgement and private AU-C 240 inquiries, kept separate from management even when one person is both (DEC-93). The agent appears nowhere, no model text reaches a client, and the firm never books an entry for an attest client.

### 3.1 C18: one way to put a decision to a client (all kinds)

*As Hannah, I want every client decision — entries, statements, packages — to reach the right client person, be decided only by them, and come back to the module that owns it.*

## 6. Accrued Agent

**None.** R-02 adds no capability, tool, prompt, model call or eval suite, so there are no eval thresholds. Guards that keep it that way:
