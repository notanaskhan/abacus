## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Ship the tier-3 layout templates and their seeded specimens before the screens that need them. A consumer epic should then build POR-06, TAX-01, ENG-04, AUD-08, EBP-04, AUD-21, NOT-03 and the 390-px portal by composing tested kits, not by inventing layout. Concretely:

- **T16 signing ceremony** (`SigningCeremony`). It works in two modes:
  - **provider mode**, the production default under DEC-25/DF-12. The firm-branded document list, review and consent steps are ours; identity verification and the signature run inside the provider's embedded view.
  - **no native mode.** Chapter 01 rules out a native KBA ceremony (§1.0, §1.1, §1.7.2), so the native artboards (T16a email code, TPL-16a KBA, TPL-16c adopt) are kept only as Tier C screenshot specimens, not components.
  
  It also covers the DF-11 handwritten path, decline and 390 px.
- **T10 document on well** (`DocumentOnWell`): structure pane with KV or clause variants, a paper card with letterhead and DRAFT watermark, cited agent-drafted blocks rendered without markup in the firm-internal `paper` variant, and the Drafting, Draft, Approved and Locked states plus an email well. The client- and agency-facing `letter` and `email` variants render template and clause blocks only while ADR P-25 is off (ADR-111, DEC-79).
- **T6 workbook / schedule grid** (`WorkbookLayout`): control bar, tally strip, statement panes, by-partner pane scroller, calc tree lit against the source workbook, Building and Signed-off states. It is built on the F-05 grammars, and the browser never sums.
- **Specimens** that lock each template against the Brightwater Hale seed at E0 (Mon Sep 14, 2026, 9:24 AM CT): TPL-01 (tax practice board), TPL-06 (Ridgeline capital accounts), TPL-10 (Ridgeline preparer memo and engagement letter), TPL-16 (Kapoor joint 8879) and TPL-19 (Feld portal on a 390-px phone).

**What a named Brightwater Hale person can do when this ships.** F-06 itself renders only in the gallery (`/components/...`) and in fixture-mode builds, so no firm user sees it directly. What the kits make possible, once each consumer wires them, is:

- **Mira Kapoor** (spouse signer, envelope E6) opens the Brightwater Hale portal on a phone and sees "Form 8879 · TY2025 — 1 of 2 signed". Mira reads what the signature authorizes (federal refund $2,296, New York refund $410), verifies identity and signs in the provider's view, then sees an evidence record of time, method, KBA result, IP, login and SHA-256 hashes. Mira can never act on Dev Kapoor's fields. After three failed KBA attempts Mira is told the firm will send a paper form; there is no "try again tomorrow". This becomes live with N-02 at **M13 (Aug 31, 2027)**.
- **Claire Feld** photographs a two-page 1095-C on a phone and attaches it to the right request, then sees "✓ You uploaded Sep 14 · Matched to this request". The upload screens at 390 px go live with R-01 for M10 (Jul 13, 2027). Server-side stitching of multi-page photos is R-03's (2029).
- **Hannah Leclerc** opens Tax Returns and filters to Awaiting signature with one tile. Per-signer chips show who still has to sign, and each due date explains its own rule on hover. This is TAX-01 in the tax pilot (T-02, M11 Aug 17, 2027), built from TPL-01.

Samuel approves it through the sign-off dialog, and the paper then reads "Approved by Samuel Ito", never "Accrued Agent". The same kit renders the 2027 client- and agency-facing documents, ENG-04 letters (N-02), NOT-03 notice responses (N-04), AUD-21 rep letters (A-07) and POR-20 emails (R-01), from templates, clause toggles and human-typed text only: no model-drafted text reaches a client or the IRS until ADR P-25 is accepted (S55) and a firm turns it on (ADR-111, DEC-79). ### F1 · Signing ceremony kit (T16, TPL-16) — T03, T04, T05, T06, T07, T09, T13

**Story 1.1 — Review and sign in provider mode (production default).** As Mira Kapoor, I review what I'm authorizing in the firm-branded ceremony, then verify and sign in the provider's view, so that Brightwater Hale can file our 2025 returns.
- **Given** envelope E6 with Dev signed Sep 9 and Mira's PIN and signature fields open, **when** the ceremony renders in provider mode (TPL-16b), **then** the envelope header reads "1 of 2 signed" (amber pill). The document list shows "✓ Dev · Sep 9" (green-tint) and "○ Mira" (neutral) on both Form 8879 and Form TR-579-IT. The steps pane shows Verify identity · Review · Sign · Done with StatusDisc discs and sub-lines, and the "What you're authorizing" card states "$2,296" and "$410".
- **Given** Mira continues to verification (TPL-16a / TPL-16c in provider mode), **when** the signing session URL arrives in `EnvelopeVM.signing_session`, **then** the page well hosts `ProviderSigningFrame`:
  - the frame has an accessible title and the sandbox attributes agreed in N-02;
  - no provider content is rendered as our markup;
  - our chrome carries no DocuSign-style branding and no Accrued mark (TPL-16 AVOID list).
- **Given** the provider returns to `/sign/$envelopeId` with any event parameter, **when** the server still reports Mira's signer as unsigned, **then** the kit shows its pending-confirmation state and **never** Completed. Only `signer.status = signed` from the server, refetched by the L3 container, moves to TPL-16d. Return parameters are hints; polling is truth (ADR-041 pattern, ch. 03 §3.9).

**Story 1.2 — One spouse cannot act for the other (INV-4).**
- **Given** Mira is the viewer of a joint envelope, **when** TPL-16b renders, **then** Dev's fields are read-only and show "Dev Kapoor · signed electronically Sep 9, 2026". Only fields whose `signer_id` equals the viewer's are focusable or actionable. "Sign here" moves focus to the viewer's next empty field and never to another signer's.
- **Given** an `EnvelopeVM` whose viewer is not a signer on the envelope, **when** the ceremony renders, **then** it shows the `restricted` view state and no page or field.

**Story 1.3 — Completion and the evidence record.**
- **Given** both signers have signed, **when** TPL-16d / T16c render, **then** the steps pane becomes the completion card (olive ✓ tile 34, "Signed. Thank you, Mira.") with six evidence rows: Signed (Sep 14, 2026, 9:24 AM CT) · Method · Identity result · IP address · Login · Documents ("SHA-256 recorded"). The envelope pill reads Completed (green), and "Download" calls `onDownload` (signed PDFs + certificate are served by N-02).
- **Given** any state, **then** SSNs render only as the masked strings the server sends. The kit has no reveal affordance (the signer packet carries no full SSN, ch. 06 §6.3).

**Story 1.4 — Three failed KBA attempts route to a handwritten signature (DF-11).**
- **Given** an 8879-family envelope whose signer status is `verification_failed`, **when** TPL-16e renders, **then** the card shows the ShieldAlert tile and "We couldn't verify your identity." with the **DF-11 text**: the firm will send a paper form, and Dina Sato is named as the contact. There is **no** retry button, **no** countdown and **no** alternative method (SMS is never offered for the 8879 family).
- **Given** a letter, consent or rep-letter envelope, **then** the identity step shows method `email_code` (T16a), not KBA (DF-11 identity per envelope type).

**Story 1.5 — Decline with a reason.**
- **Given** Mira opens "Decline…" (TPL-16f), **when** the dialog renders, **then** it offers the four reasons ("The amounts look wrong · I have a question first · Someone else should sign · Other") plus a note. "Decline" (destructive-ink) is disabled until a reason is chosen. Confirming calls `onDecline({reason, note})` exactly once, and focus returns to the trigger. The firm-side Declined chip in SIG-01 belongs to N-02.

**Story 1.6 — Signing on a phone (TPL-16g, TPL-19 frame 9).**
- **Given** a 390 × 844 viewport, **when** TPL-16g renders, **then** the 56 px header, the step chips row "Verify ✓ · Review ✓ · Sign · Done", the full-width page with a pinch hint and the 64 px sticky bar with a 48 px primary all fit with no horizontal scroll. ## 6. Accrued Agent

**None.** F-06 declares no capability, registers no prompt, calls no tool, makes no `ai_gateway` call and adds no eval suite or threshold.

The kits *display* other epics' agent work, under these rules:
- **Drafting and Building** (T10a, TPL-10b, T6c, TPL-06d) render from a `status` or run-state prop (C6 v1 read API, mapped in L3). The kits do not depend on the E6 run store, and no scripted run is needed for parity (`?static=1`).

T17 asserts it.

- **Attribution** uses `formatActor`: "Adopted by Olivia Brandt", "Approved by Samuel Ito", never "Accrued Agent" as an approver (INV-5).
- **Signing has no agent at all** (T16 digest: "None — signing is human-only").
