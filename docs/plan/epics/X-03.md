## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Let a firm deliver co-sourced internal audit (D2) to its clients on Accrued: a risk-based annual plan that the client's audit committee approves in the portal, risk and control matrices with cited walkthrough narratives on Sheets, issues written the way the IIA Global Internal Audit Standards expect (condition, criteria, cause, effect, recommendation) with client-owned action plans, firm validation by re-test, and a committee dashboard and quarterly pack that foot to the issue register. BSA/AML independent testing rides the same screens as a template (X-01b). Every count is computed by code; the Accrued Agent drafts and proposes; named people rate, send, validate, share and approve.
