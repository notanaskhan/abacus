## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Replace CLI tenant provisioning and entitlement edits with an Accrued staff console. Give each firm a plan-and-usage page in agent-minutes, with alerts and a partner-approved "Request more". Put an in-app Support drawer (human chat, help articles, system status) in front of every firm user. Make Accrued staff access to firm content strictly request-only, firm-approved, scoped to one object and time-boxed, by amending SPEC-012 as DEC-72 decided. The epic adds no billing, no prices and no payments (P-27, D5).

**What named people can do when it ships** (Brightwater Hale seed; demo clock Mon Sep 14, 2026, 9:24 AM CT unless stated):
