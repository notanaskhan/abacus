## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Give Brightwater Hale's governance owners the three screens that let a firm prove, to a peer reviewer, a cyber-insurer or the FTC, that **every AI output was approved by a named human under a written, versioned policy, that its people sign in the way the Safeguards Rule requires, and that every change left an immutable, attributable trace**. Concretely:

1. **ADM-07 Audit log** — one firm-wide, immutable, filterable log of every event with the actor grammar "‹person›", "‹person› via Agent", "Policy: ‹name vN›" or "System", never "Accrued Agent" as an approver; the IP, device and session behind each human event; before and after values resolved **at read time** and masked for anyone without content rights; and an export with a signed manifest.
2. **ADM-06 Security** — sign-in enforcement (SSO, mandatory MFA, session and idle limits, the one SSO-exempt emergency account), the portal sign-in floor (email code, MFA for owners, signers, approvers and anyone who sees tax return information), active sessions, the firm's FTC incident register with its 30-day clock, the WISP and IRS Pub 4557 evidence, US-only residency, read-only retention floors, the dual-control firm data export and the support-access switch.
3. **ADM-04 AI policy v1** — the versioned "BH AI policy vN": the twelve approval gates (who approves each kind of agent output), the autonomy level, Auto mode defaults per engagement type (stored; forced off for real firms until runtime v2), the §7216 rule, the sub-processors generated from the provider-terms register, agent governance; plus three immediate controls outside versioning: the firm's client-data opt-in (SPEC-036), each agent's shadow/live stage (read-only) and **Pause all agents**.
