## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Let firm staff keep working in Excel and Word without losing what makes Accrued trustworthy. In **Excel**, a workbook opened from Accrued stays bound to its engagement and procedure: cells link to evidence as verified citations, the firm's standard tickmarks are recorded with who and when, and the preparer sign-off is **the same `sign_offs` row** that SHL-12 writes, signed over a write-once copy of the workbook that Accrued stores and hashes. An edit after sign-off turns the slot stale and can only be cleared by signing again. In **Word**, a statements document generated by Accrued (FSP-06) stays current: values that changed in Accrued arrive as numbered incoming changes, and one person-initiated **Update document** replaces only the linked values, leaving formatting, comments and tracked changes alone. After release (G8) the copy is read-only. Nothing in the add-ins is decided by the agent, nothing is written while offline, and nothing typed in Excel or Word changes Accrued's data.

### F1 · Sign in from Office, and the add-in surface (XLA-01f2, XLA-01f3, XLA-02e)

**US-1.1** As Cole, I sign in with my firm's SSO from inside Excel.
- *Given* the pane opens with no session, *then* it shows only "Sign in to Accrued", "Use your firm's single sign-on. Links, tickmarks and sign-offs stay with the workpaper." and the primary **Sign in with Brightwater Hale SSO**, with the firm name read from the document's `AccruedFirm` custom property written at generation, never a constant; a document without it reads "Sign in with your firm's SSO" (XLA-01f2; DF-30).
- *When* Cole presses it, *then* `Office.context.ui.displayDialogAsync` opens `dialog.html` on the add-in origin, which runs the existing PKCE flow (`apps/web/src/auth/pkce.ts` logic, copied with its tests) against the same IdP and posts only the authorization result back with `messageParent`; the pane completes the PKCE code exchange with the IdP, calls `POST /v1/addin/session` with that IdP access token as `Authorization: Bearer` (an ordinary authenticated `AbacusRouter` route, never an unauthenticated one) and keeps the opaque session **in memory only** (never `localStorage`, Office settings or the document).
