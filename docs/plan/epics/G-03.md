## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Let a firm build on Accrued **without a back door**, and let it shape its own data model **without weakening classification**:

1. **API keys and a public read API v1**: firm-owned, read-only, expiring, IP-restricted keys whose rights are the key's scopes intersected with its creator's live rights, over a small, versioned, metadata-only API with a published reference.
2. **Webhooks (W8)**: identifier-only events from the outbox, HMAC-signed, delivered at least once through a separate SSRF-safe egress proxy, with a visible delivery health and a manual retry.
3. **An MCP server**: a person connects their own AI assistant (Claude first) with OAuth; the assistant acts **as that person**, read-only, inside their walls, with masked fields that cannot be revealed, return information withheld under §7216, IT/Security approving each new connection, and every tool call in the audit log. Accrued Agent is never the actor.
4. **The firm's data model**: custom fields on clients, engagements, requests and controls, each with a **mandatory classification** that is enforced at runtime in screens, logs, exports, external surfaces and agent context; tags; and a read view of the system adjustment types and trial balance labels with the firm's defaults and rule toggles.

**What Brightwater Hale people can do when this ships.** Staging, with the synthetic tenant, from S72; production canary from **Tue Aug 14, 2029** (the four journeys pass there by M23, Fri Aug 17); real firms from **Tue Oct 23, 2029**, after the window, or earlier on request through the P-29 checklist (§10).

### 4.5 Temporal workflows and activities

- **`WebhookDeliveryWorkflow`** (`webhook_delivery:<tenant>:<delivery>`, class `time_sensitive`), declared in `modules/integrations/webhooks/workflows.py` and started by the outbox subscription through `kernel.dispatch` (DISPATCH-001). Attempts at 0, +1 min and +10 min with durable timers; each attempt is activity `integrations.deliver_webhook(tenant, delivery_id, n)`, which opens the sealed secret only inside the activity, signs, posts through the egress proxy and records the attempt and status in its own unit of work. After attempt 3 fails: delivery `failed`, endpoint `failing`, outbox `webhook_endpoint.status_changed`. Retry now signals the workflow (or starts a new one for a closed delivery). Replay history recorded under `backend/tests/workflows/histories/webhook-delivery-v1-*.json` (ADR-090).
- **`ApiKeyExpirySweep`** (Temporal Schedule per firm, 06:00 firm time zone, `background`): expires keys and emits the 30- and 7-day notices once each.
- **`DeveloperPurge`** (daily, `batch`): expired `mcp_tokens` and `mcp_authorization_requests` older than 24 h; `webhook_deliveries` and attempts past 90 days and `api_request_events` past 1 year through the P-14 retention clock (they are record types, so legal holds apply).

## 6. Accrued Agent
