### 3.1 AI on a firm's real data: opt-in, stages, exclusion (TASK-076)

*As Grace Holloway (Firm Admin), I turn AI on for Brightwater Hale's clients' data after reading who processes it, so the firm decides with the facts in front of it. As Daniel Reyes, I can switch AI off for one engagement. As N, I promote an assistant from shadow to live only with evidence.*

- **AC-1** *Given* a non-synthetic firm without an opt-in, *when* any specialist calls the gateway on the Bedrock route, *then* the call is refused with reason `firm` before any network call (a stub provider proves it), `ai.boundary_refused` is audited, and the work is never retried or rerouted (SPEC-036 AC-2).
- **AC-2** *Given* Grace with a second factor older than the `mfa_recent` window, *when* Grace opts in, *then* the API answers 401 `mfa_required`; with a fresh factor the firm records `ai_client_data_at`, `_by` and `_version` (the compiled register hash), and the audit row reads "AI on clients' data turned on by Grace Holloway". Any other role is refused (SPEC-036 AC-3).
- **AC-3** *Given* the disclosure route, *then* it returns the provider, region, training, retention and sub-processors **from `kernel/_provider_terms.py`** and each specialist with its stage; it never contains "Anthropic" as the processor (conflict C-03, DF-16), feeding ADM-04 h.
- **AC-4** *Given* an opted-in firm and an agent with no stage row, *then* the agent's stage is `off`; the first stage the platform action can set is `shadow`; `live` is refused unless the stage has been `shadow` for that firm (SPEC-036 §4.5).
- **AC-5** *Given* an agent in `shadow` for a firm, *when* it runs, *then* usage, outputs and check results are recorded, and every firm-facing read (screening results on AUD-03, upload-review flags, inbox match proposals, guidance drafts, human-gate counts on HOME-01) returns nothing from those runs; screens behave as if the agent were off (SPEC-036 AC-5).
- **AC-6** *Given* Daniel excludes an engagement with a reason, *then* every agent call on it is refused (`engagement`), the exclusion is audited with Daniel as actor, and lifting it is audited too (SPEC-036 AC-6, tested in T09). The engagement's screens then say AI is off for it, and the opt-in, stage and exclusion controls have loading, empty, error and not-allowed states (SPEC-036 AC-6 screen half and AC-9): built by G-02 (ADM-04, G-02-T25) and P-07 (the exclusion row, §2.4), tested by Q-04-T351 before M9.
- **AC-7** *Given* the register's Bedrock entry is replaced with one that changes training, retention, region or sub-processors, *then* every opted-in firm reads `opt_in_required` and is refused (`firm`) until it opts in again; a change of `review_by` alone does not reset it (SPEC-036 Q4).
- **AC-8** *Given* opt-out, *then* the next call in the handling process is refused, workers refuse at the next run start (outbox event `firm.ai_client_data_changed` clears their caches), and in-flight calls elsewhere stop within the 60-second cache period (SPEC-036 Q5).
- **AC-9** *Given* a real firm with every setting on, *then* no model-written text reaches any client principal (SPEC-036 AC-7; ADR-111).

### 6.3 Shadow scoring (T50)

The shadow report compares each shadow output with what the firm's people decided on the same item (screening outcome vs the reviewer's accept or return; match proposal vs where staff filed the document). It reports agreement, the override rate as defined in chapter 05 §5.5.7 ((rejected + edited) ÷ decided, at least 25 outputs) and the cases that disagreed, and it marks pass or fail for the two promotion criteria: the SPEC-036 Q1 floor (≥ 50 runs and ≥ 14 days, whichever is later) and chapter 10 **QD-10** (≥ 25 scored decisions, disagreement ≤ 8%, zero DE-1, DE-4 or DE-5 misses on items the human returned or refused). It is a deterministic, versioned export whose SHA-256 the stage change records, and `platform.agent_stage.set` refuses `live` unless the named report passes both (Q-03 I3 and Q-04 AC-4.6 rely on this). It is the evidence behind each Jul 13 promotion and feeds F-09's Resource Pack v3 later.
