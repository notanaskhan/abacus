> Epic specification and task backlog, written Sunday 2026-10-18 for Sprint 0 starting Monday 2026-10-19. Task references `Tnn` in the text mean `X-01-Tnn`. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for dates, owners and scope. Chapter 09 is the register of record for `DEC-nn`, `DF-nn` and `P-nn` IDs; this spec never cites chapter 03's or chapter 06's own P-34+ numbers. >
> **Short version.** X-01 opens the attest service line (decision D1): the firm acts as **service auditor** under AT-C 105/205 and issues its own SOC report. It ships ten P1 screens on engines that already exist by S62:
> 1. **KNW-09 Frameworks** and **KNW-10 Control set and cross-map**: a versioned framework library with licence gating (ADR-056, D8), firm paraphrase plus IDs only, and one control set mapped once to several frameworks with coverage computed by code.
