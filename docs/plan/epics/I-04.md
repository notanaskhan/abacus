## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Put the money and the people next to the work, without Accrued becoming a billing or timekeeping system. **Budgets** are owned in Accrued by the engagement manager and approved by the engagement partner; **actual time, invoices, payments, write-downs and standard rates are read** from the firm's practice-management system (CCH Axcess Practice), so WIP, AR aging and realization are computed by code beside the engagement; the **agents' effort** (agent-minutes) sits beside the human hours and is never billable. **Capacity** is a people × weeks plan owned in Accrued: managers move work before a deadline slips or someone passes 40 hours, and unassigned work goes only to people whose independence is on file. Nothing here is decided by the agent; nothing is invoiced, paid or written back to practice management.

### 4.11 Compute (code computes, ADR-050)

`modules/practice/compute/economics.py` and `compute/capacity.py` are pure functions over typed decimals (`kernel.numeric`), with Hypothesis property tests (footing, conservation, idempotence) and **golden fixtures written by AY** (T22; chapter 03 §10 rule: the founder who did not implement an engine writes its goldens, and N implements the economics compute in T11 and T12 after the rebalance; N's suite A covers AY's capacity compute).

| Function | Definition |
|---|---|
| `eac(actual, etc?, budget)` | `actual + (etc if etc is not None else max(0, budget − actual))`, per person × phase; role lines without a person use the remaining role budget |
| `standard_value(hours_by_person, rate_set)` | Σ hours × level rate; EP and EQR at the partner rate; seasonal at staff; non-chargeable at 0 |
| `realization_projected` | fee ÷ standard value at EAC, half-up to 0.1% (32,000 ÷ 39,390 = 81.2%) |
| `realization_final` | billed ÷ standard value to date (96,000 ÷ 118,400 = 81.1%) |
| `budget_change` | net = Σ Δ; gross % = Σ \|Δcell\| ÷ approved total × 100, half-up to 0.1 (4 ÷ 172 = 2.3%) (OQ-2) |
| `wip_row` | unbilled WIP = std value to date − billed − write-down; AR = billed − collected; aging by days since invoice date at the firm-TZ clock date; overdue = unpaid past `due_on` |
| `capacity_week` | capacity = weekly hours if the week's Monday ≤ season end else 0; total = Σ allocation h/wk; over = total > capacity; free = max(0, capacity − total) |
| `utilization` | Σ used ÷ Σ capacity over shown people for the week, half-up to 1% (330 ÷ 480 = 69%) |
| `fit(need, person)` | free per need week; `fits` if every week's free ≥ need h/wk, else `over` with n = weeks short |
| `review_hours(spans)` | Σ span seconds per person ÷ 3,600, half-up to 0.5 h; totals sum rounded values |
