# N-04 · Notices

> Epic specification and task backlog, written Sunday 2026-10-18 for the plan whose Sprint 0 starts Monday 2026-10-19. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for dates, owners and scope. Decision and ADR IDs are chapter 09's (DEC-nn, ADR P-nn); repo ADR numbers in brackets are the reservations in F-02's schedule. **AY** = ayushkbhatia, **N** = notanaskhan. The CSV twin of §9 is `sections/epics/N-04.tasks.csv`.
>
> **Short version.** N-04 ships Notices as the first year-round, tax-adjacent module, live for the pilot firms at **M15, Fri Dec 10, 2027** (chapter 01: "J09 (notices) P0 live"). Three screens carry it:
> 1. **NOT-01 Notices index and intake sheet.** Every open IRS and state notice, sorted by deadline risk, with what is due within 7 days and which have no authorization on file. A new notice arrives from an upload, the firm inbox, the portal or an organizer answer, already read (P-11 OCR and code box maps, or the Accrued Agent for notice types without a map) and matched to the client, the return and the authorization on file. A person confirms it and never retypes it.
> 2. **NOT-02 Notice workspace.** The notice, the return as filed and the information returns compared line by line **by code**; a cited explanation and a recommended position from the Accrued Agent where the firm has cleared it; the agree / partially agree / disagree position decided only by the signing partner.
> 3. **NOT-03 Response letter.** The taxpayers' letter from a firm template held in the database, prepared → reviewed → approved by the partner, signed by the taxpayers through a C7 envelope, paid by the taxpayers outside Accrued (link-out, ADR P-27), sent by a person through the IRS Documentation Upload Tool, fax or mail and recorded, then tracked with a 60-day follow-up to resolution.
>
> **What N-04 never does.** The agent never decides a position, signs off, sends, contacts an agency or files anything (INV-1). No model-drafted text reaches the IRS: ADR P-25 is accepted (Decision Day, chapter 01 §1.0) but off per firm until a drafter clears its evals, and no notices drafter is cleared, so letters come from templates plus human edits. Accrued never takes a payment, stores a payment instrument or transmits to the IRS (ADR P-27, requirement R-04).

## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal (chapter 01 §1.4.3).** "Notices as the first year-round tax-adjacent module once the Sec. 7216 gate exists: intake by upload, inbox or portal with OCR extraction, code comparison against the return as filed, partner decision, response letter with provenance and signature, follow-up timer; payments as link-outs only."

**What Brightwater Hale people can do at epic exit (Fri Dec 10, 2027).** On staging this runs on the synthetic tenant under the demo clock; the artboards' clocks are Thu Sep 10, 2026 2:16 PM (intake), Mon Sep 14, 2026 8:58 AM – 10:14 AM CT (analysis to approval), Sep 16–17 (signed, paid, sent), Nov 16 (follow-up) and Dec 2, 2026 (resolved).
