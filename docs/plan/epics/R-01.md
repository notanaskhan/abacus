## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal (chapter 01 §1.4.3).** Ship the portal PBC core and the white-label surfaces that bring clients into it: documents with drop-anywhere sorting, ignore-with-reason and Mark as Done; the request sheet; a request-scoped third-party room for TPAs, recordkeepers, custodians and banks; the transactional email templates as versioned database content; branding slots; and a per-firm sender domain.
