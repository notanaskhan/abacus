## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Spend the 22.5% hardening reserve so that every claim the plan makes at a gate is true when it is made, and stays true for 2½ years of live use:
1. **Pay back the 55 owed ADR-078 suites before real data** (chapter 01 §1.4.3; chapter 10 §10.3): 44 back-filled black-box by AY's sessions in S6–S11 (tranche 0 already done in S0–S3 under QD-2), 11 superseded: seven closed by their replacing epics' merged AC suites and the four whose replacing epic lands after M8 (023, 053, 061, 063) back-filled black-box on their legacy paths in S14 (T524, T525), every suite re-run against the final MVP contracts in S12, coverage ratcheted to 97% line on red modules in S13, all 55 closed at **M8**.
2. **Prove the tests catch bugs** with mutation testing on red modules and compute packages (ADR-079): wiring in S8, floors at the S11 baseline (QD-4), survivor sweeps every quarter.
3. **Close every pen-test finding before the surface touches real data**: pen test #1 (Feb 15 – Mar 5, 2027; highs retested closed by Apr 9), delta #1 (Jun 21 – Jul 2), delta #2 (Aug 2–11), pen test #2 (Nov 2027), and the 2028–2029 deltas and annual test that the feature epics schedule. Each finding becomes a regression test written by the founder who did not write the fix (chapter 10 §10.10.3).
4. **Load-test every season before it starts**: ×25 before M7, M10 and M13; ×100 in November 2027 and November 2028 (ADR-075); ×25–×100 before the Sep 1, 2028 window; a ×25 GA regression in S70 (Jun 25 – Jul 6, 2029). Never against a database a hosted build is using.
5. **Prove recovery every quarter**: eleven restore drills (2027 Q1 – 2029 Q3), eleven kill-switch game days, a yearly regional failover game day, expand-only migration rehearsals before each window, and two drills run by AY alone (ADR-100).
6. **Keep the SOC 2 Type II controls operating** through year 1 (May 1 – Jul 31, 2027), year 2 (Aug 2027 – Jul 2028) and year 3 (Aug 2028 – Jul 2029), with the FTC Safeguards annual items.
7. **Keep the assurance machinery honest**: contract and connector suites, generated security tests, supply chain, route parity under 90 days, agent accuracy samples, journey and parity reliability, and the quarterly reserve review that keeps the reserve off features (chapter 01 §1.8.6 rule 1).
