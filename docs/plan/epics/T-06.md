# T-06 · Entity return depth, relationships & hand-offs (P1)

*Epic specification and task backlog, written Sunday 2026-10-18 for Sprint 0 starting Monday 2026-10-19. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for order, dates, owners and scope. Chapter 09 is the register of record for `DEC-nn`, `DF-nn` and `P-nn` IDs; this spec never cites the conflicting P-34+ numbers minted in chapters 03 and 06. **N** = notanaskhan, **AY** = ayushkbhatia. The machine-readable backlog is `sections/epics/T-06.tasks.csv`; §9 mirrors it row for row. Repo paths are relative to `github.com/notanaskhan/abacus` at `a42034c`. Paths marked \* do not exist yet (this epic or the upstream epic named beside them creates them); every other path was checked in the clone.*

> **Short version.** T-06 turns the 1065 workspace that **T-05** shipped for the 2028 extension season into a complete entity-return practice for the 2029 season, and joins tax to audit and to the client graph. It ships eight P1 screens:
> 1. **The rest of the 1065 review** — fixed assets and MACRS (TXP-03), Schedules M-3, M-1 and M-2 (TXP-04), inside and outside basis with §752 shares and the §754 decision sent to the client (TXP-08), and states with apportionment and PTET (TXP-09). Each runs on a pure, golden-tested engine (E3, E4, E8, E9).
> 2. **Other entity types** — 1120-S AAA, 1120 ASC 740 provision and 990 Part IX in the same tally-strip grammar (TXP-13), on engines E10–E12.
> 3. **Form preview with K-1s** (TAX-11) — a watermarked, read-only face of the return rendered by the C11 renderer from an approved checkpoint (DEC-78), with code diagnostics that block final approval until they clear.
> 4. **Relationships map** (CLI-03) — the household and ownership graph on T14, built from the TPL-14 specimen because the screen has no board, with the "Ridgeline 1065 → 2 K-1s at risk" dependency banner.
> 5. **Audit → tax hand-off** (AUD-38) — after release, the audited Final TB lands in the client's Form 1120 workpapers with tax codes, schedules and tax-relevant items. Nothing posts back. T-06 **provides contract C12**, which **C-02** also uses for the close → 1120-S hand-off (CLS-12).
>
> **The agent never decides** (INV-1). It extracts dates and percentages with verified citations, proposes sourcing positions and tax codes, recommends on §754 and explains differences. Named people approve changes, approve sourcing, send the §754 question, hand off, create relationships and give final approval. The client's partners decide §754.
