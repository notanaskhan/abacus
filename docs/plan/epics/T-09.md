>
> **Short version.** T-09 ships the advisory line on spines that already exist by Apr 2029. It has three parts:
> 1. **Proposals (ENG-09).** A partner turns opportunities raised from a return into priced, scoped options (fixed, value or hourly), sends them, and the client accepts online. Acceptance never creates an invoice or takes a payment (ADR P-27). Convert creates the engagement, a draft letter and, for advisory work, a project.
> 2. **The project workspace (PRJ-01).** Milestones and deliverables whose states are computed from the objects they are bound to; a partner-reserved decision card; agent runs that model the §743(b) step-up with code-computed figures; and client actions that respect G3, the host engagement and the §7216 consent for a named third party.
> 3. **Templates and research (KNW-05).** Cited research memos and firm positions with a citation check that must pass before a partner publishes, and a read-and-bind view over P-11's database templates and generated documents.
>
> **The Accrued Agent prepares, cites and waits.** It models, drafts and recommends. It never decides, publishes, sends, releases, converts or accepts anything (INV-1). Every figure it shows comes from a compute call (ADR-050). Every citation is verified by code before a partner can rely on it (INV-2).

### 2.2 In scope

1. **Fee proposals** (`practice`): drafts with options, assumptions, exclusions and terms; code-computed totals; Circular 230 §10.27 validation; sources from planning opportunities with T-08's consent basis (C19); send as an immutable version with a content hash and a C11 PDF; viewed, withdrawn, revised, expired; portal accept and decline bound to the sent version; Convert to an engagement of the proposal's type, a draft letter and, for advisory work, a project.
2. **Advisory projects** (`engagements`): status Scoping · Active · Delivered · Closed; milestones and deliverables whose states are derived from bound objects; to-dos plus linked work rows; runs; the inline firm-recommendation decision card; the §754 question through the return's client election card; project questions and deliverable release under an explicit **host engagement**; the engineer request held on G3 and a named-third-party §7216 consent; the blocked-on-client banner; the hand-off of the adopted model to the return as a reviewer-approved change set.
3. **Research memos and firm positions** (`methodology`): insert-only section versions; citation check by code, including a new `authority_section` verifier over a public authority corpus held as platform content; partner-only publish bound to a passing check; links to returns, projects and issues; Raise decision; KNW-01 catalog providers.
4. **Templates tab** (`reporting` consumer): list, bound preview, versions and compare, generated documents with signature status and out-of-date reasons; regenerate through P-11's human-only route.
5. **Advisory compute library** (`tax`): pure functions with golden tests and CPA review, registered as compute tools; the firm-recommendation decision kind provider; the `section_743b_adjustment` proposal kind for the return hand-off.
6. **Two agent capabilities** (`agents`, red): `advisory.analyst` (modes `step_up_model`, `cost_seg_scoping`) and `knowledge.research_memo` (modes `research_memo`, `recommendation_memo`), with eval suites, Bedrock clearance and library card 5.
7. **Content as database rows**: the authority corpus, the advisory project template, the proposal and advisory-memo report templates and the proposal email template, loaded through import APIs or the platform library release, never commits.
