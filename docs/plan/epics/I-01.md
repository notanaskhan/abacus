# I-01 · Home & approvals queue

> Epic specification and task backlog for the plan dated Sat 2026-10-17; Sprint 0 starts Mon 2026-10-19. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for order, dates, owners and scope; chapter 09 is the register of record for `DEC-nn`, `P-nn` (proposed ADR) and `DF-nn` IDs. **AY** = ayushkbhatia, **N** = notanaskhan. The machine-readable backlog is `sections/epics/I-01.tasks.csv`; §9 mirrors it row for row.
>
> **Short version.** I-01 makes Home the firm app's landing page and gives every person one queue of the human gates that wait on them.
> 1. **HOME-01** (T11 dashboard): role-shaped tiles, "Waiting on you", the viewer's deadlines and work, client activity and a running-agents pill. It ships first as **HOME-01 lite** for MVP-1 (code-complete at M8, Fri May 28, 2027; internal shadow from Tue Jun 1, 2027).
> 2. **HOME-02** (T9 close-review): the approvals and review queue with the selected gate's detail and evidence beside it. It ships once P-04's review-grammar UI lands (SHL-10 S16, SHL-12 S19, SHL-11 S20–S21), merges dark through the Sep 1 – Oct 20, 2027 window, and flips per firm in the Tue Nov 2 and Nov 9, 2027 windows.
>
> Both screens read **one count source**, `GET /v1/human-gates/counts`, so "11 = 3 + 6 + 2" is the same number on the HOME-01 header, the Approvals badge, the HOME-02 bands, the SHL-04 Approvals tab and, later, the I-02 Today brief. I-01 co-provides contract **C13** with N (P-04): N owns the projection, the slot and the counts service (red `review`); AY owns the home read model, the band rule and the frontend gate-class registry (amber `practice`, `platform`).

**External systems.** I-01 calls none. Home deep-links to surfaces that do:

| Firm mailbox (M365) | Activity rows → INB-01 (N-01) | Fake mailbox on staging; activity source added S29 |

**Obligations.**
- **§7216 / TRI.** Tax gate rows carry return facts (e.g. "proposed tax 686.40"). They are resolved at read time by the tax owner's resolver, which applies the TRI consent and offshore conditions per row; nothing from Home is sent to a model, logged, put in a URL or stored in `human_gates` (identifiers and enums only). Offshore members without the disclosure consent see the row without TRI facts or not at all, per the owner's rule.
- **Need-to-know and walls.** ADR-024 (admins: metadata and counts), ADR-026 and DEC-43 (walls, 404, no counts), DEC-30 (office is not a boundary). Tested in T03 and T09.
- **Agents propose, humans decide.** ADR-005 at matrix, service and database (INV-1); every gate verb opens its owner's overlay; nothing is decided, signed, approved or sent from Home's own endpoints.
- **Client content is hostile.** Activity facts are schema-validated enums and counts; names render from organisation records; no client-supplied string is stored by Home.
- **Retention.** `human_gates` and `home_activity_items` are rebuildable projections, not records (90-day prune for activity). Snooze and reassignment rows are insert-only and kept with their audit events under the tenant's default retention floor.
- **Audit and SOC 2.** Reassignment is an access-relevant change and is audited with actor and reason; reads of Home are not audited (no sealed content).
- **Busy-season freeze (ADR P-29, DEC-68).** No live-path change Sep 1 – Oct 20, 2027 and Jan 1 – Apr 20, 2028: HOME-02 code merges dark in S22–S23, wires up after Oct 20 (T21), flips per firm in Tuesday windows (T22); the h/i bands are built in S35 as new, unwired components shown only in fixture mode (T24) and wired into live HOME-01 after Apr 20, 2028 by T27 (Tue Apr 25 train, per-firm Tuesday flips through Q-04); both I-01 flags are retired before Jan 1, 2028.
- **E-sign, ERO, client writes:** Home never signs, transmits or writes to client systems; connectors stay read-only (AGENTS.md).
