## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Ship the firm-wide "Go…" command palette (SHL-02) over a permission-aware Postgres index, and freeze **C15 v1**, the search registration contract, so that every module from S39 onwards makes its objects findable by registering a projection rather than editing the platform. The index stores metadata only. Every group is filtered by the same read action and scope kind as the screen it opens, so a result never 404s when it is opened. Walls are evaluated at query time, and the API returns no hidden-result count, no suggestion and no ranking signal derived from anything the viewer cannot see (DEC-43).

**What Brightwater Hale people can do when this ships** (staging and the canary at the end of S41; per-firm production flips on Tuesdays after that):

- **Hannah Leclerc** (Audit Senior Manager) presses ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows) or clicks **Go…** on any firm screen. With nothing typed she sees **Recent** (her last five opened records) and **Create** (SHL-02b). She types "feld" and, within about a quarter of a second, sees grouped results in one canonical order: *Clients* (Jonah & Claire Feld · Household · Brooklyn, NY · TY2025 Form 1040), *Returns* (2025 Form 1040 "In review · Manager"; the Ridgeline 2025 Form 1065 with the context "Jonah Feld, limited partner 25%"), *Binders* (2025 Binder · 2 open requests · "Published"), *Files* (Claire_Feld_2025_W2_MercerHill.pdf · "Approved"), *Inbox* (Re: 2025 tax documents · "Waiting") and *Actions* (New engagement for Jonah & Claire Feld…, New to-do on Jonah & Claire Feld…). ↑/↓ moves, ↵ opens the record, ⌘↵ opens it in a new tab, and Esc returns focus to Go… (SHL-02a).
- Typing "granite" on Mon Sep 28 shows Granite Peak Outdoor Supply, Inc., its FY2026 Financial Statement Audit ("Fieldwork") and Cole Hart's to-do "Re-request 2 SURL files" due Sep 28. ↵ opens that to-do in SHL-17 (SHL-02f).
- A typo such as "pellstret" gives the empty state and "Did you mean Pell Street II LLC · partner in Ridgeline Property Partners, LP?", computed only from records Hannah may open (SHL-02d).
- **Daniel Reyes** (Tax Partner) types "ridgeline" on Monday morning and lands on the Ridgeline 1065 in the entity-return workspace (TXP-01, once T-05 has registered it; until then the 1065 row opens the client record) or the client record (CLI-02) (J13 step 4, J16 step 9).
- **Lena Park**, walled from Ashford, types "ashford" and sees exactly what she would see for a client that does not exist: no row, no count, no suggestion, no different footer (SHL-02e as amended by DEC-43; INV-3).
- **Grace Holloway** (Firm Admin) sees only the results her metadata-only rights let her open (ADR-024). Offshore staff never see tax-return results for a taxpayer without an offshore-disclosure consent (§7216). Client users never get a palette: it is not in the portal bundle, and `search.read` refuses every client role (INV-4).

## 3. Features & user stories

### 3.1 Find and open a record

**Story 1.1.** As Hannah Leclerc, I type part of a client, form, period, file name or identifier and jump to it in two keystrokes.
- *Given* E0 seed and Hannah signed in with `search.palette` on, *when* she presses ⌘K and types "feld", *then* SHL-02a renders the groups in canonical order with at most 5 rows each, every row with a type tile, title, context after "·" and a canonical status word from `status.ts` (Clients have none, Files show the olive ✓ disc "Approved", Inbox the amber dot "Waiting"), and the matched substring at weight 500.
- *Given* SHL-02a, *when* she presses ↓ to the 2025 Form 1040 and ↵, *then* TAX-02 opens for that return and the palette closes; *when* she presses ⌘↵ instead, *then* the return opens in a new tab, already signed in (R-5), and the palette stays open.
- *Given* the 1065 for Ridgeline Property Partners, LP lists Jonah Feld as a 25% limited partner, *when* she types "feld", *then* the 1065 row shows the context "Jonah Feld, limited partner 25%", and ↵ opens TXP-01 (1065 family), not TAX-02.
- *Given* TXP-01 is still `planned` in the screen manifest (T-05 registers it within S35–S44), *then* every entity-return row (1065, 1120-S, 1041) routes in production to its client record (CLI-02), and in dev and fixture builds to the "Not built yet" state (chapter 04 §2.1 rule 4). The J13 step 4 assertion on the TXP-01 landing stays `test.fixme("T-05: TXP-01")` until T-05 lands; the CLI-02 half of the step runs.
- *Given* any result, *when* it is opened, *then* the target never answers 404 or 403 for that viewer (property test "every result opens", T09).
- *Given* an identifier such as `BH-FS-430` or `2025I:FeldJ:V3`, *when* it is typed as a prefix, *then* the record it identifies ranks first in its group.

**Story 1.2.** As Hannah on Mon Sep 28, I find to-dos alongside records.
- *Given* the Sep 28 snapshot, *when* she types "granite", *then* SHL-02f shows Clients (Granite Peak Outdoor Supply, Inc. · C corp · Denver), Audits (FY2026 Financial Statement Audit · "Fieldwork"), To-dos ("Re-request 2 SURL files" · Cole Hart · BH-FS-430 · due chip "Sep 28", red tint only when overdue) and Actions; *when* she presses ↵ on the to-do, *then* SHL-17 opens on it (`?ov=todos:<id>`).
- *Given* any to-do row, *then* the UI never says "task" (DEC-14, L2 lint), and no to-do is ever shown as assigned to the agent.

**Story 1.3.** As Hannah, a typo still gets me there, without hinting at anything I cannot see.
- *Given* "pellstret", *when* no document matches, *then* SHL-02d shows the EmptyState copy verbatim and one suggestion row "Did you mean Pell Street II LLC · partner in Ridgeline Property Partners, LP?", and ↵ on it opens that record.
- *Given* the nearest trigram match is in a client the viewer is walled from, *when* the suggestion is computed, *then* it is drawn only from documents that pass the viewer's `visible()` predicates, so the walled title is never suggested and the response is identical to a corpus without it (INV-3).

### 3.2 Walls, need-to-know, offshore and the portal

**Story 2.1.** As Lena Park, walled from Ashford, I can search freely, and the palette reveals nothing about Ashford.
- *Given* an active wall between Lena and the Ashford client, *when* she types "ashford", *then* the palette shows SHL-02d's EmptyState with no suggestion, the API response contains no group, no `total_visible` contribution, no suggestion and no field named or meaning "hidden", and the server records no read of an Ashford object (INV-3; chapter 10 §10.6.2).
- *Given* Hannah and two Feld documents in a client she is walled from (a scenario-local wall), *when* she types "feld", *then* SHL-02e renders exactly SHL-02a: the walled rows are absent and **no footer note, lock glyph or count appears** (DEC-43, DF-14).
- *Given* a firm admin adds a wall at 10:00:00, *when* the walled person searches at 10:00:01, *then* the walled client's documents are already excluded, because walls are evaluated at query time and never baked into the index.
- *Given* any persona, *then* `total_visible` equals the number of rows the same predicates would list, capped at 99, and never counts a row the viewer cannot open.

**Story 2.2.** As Grace Holloway (Firm Admin, metadata-only per ADR-024), I see what I may open and nothing more.
- *Given* Grace holds no engagement role on the Feld 1040, *when* she types "feld", *then* the Returns group omits the 1040 if TAX-02's read action denies her, and the Clients row appears only if CLI-02's read action allows her.

**Story 2.3.** As an offshore staff member (§7216), I never see tax-return results for a taxpayer whose offshore-disclosure consent is missing.
- *Given* a membership with `offshore = true` and no `consent(offshore_disclosure)` for the Feld taxpayers, *when* that user types "feld", *then* no `data_class = tri` document for those taxpayers is returned; *given* the consent exists, *then* they are.

**Story 2.4.** As Meera Shah (client, role union), I never reach firm search.
- *Given* any client principal, *when* `POST /v1/search` or either recents route is called, *then* it answers 403 `forbidden` by role; and the portal bundle contains no palette code (chapter 04 §1.7 import boundary; production `dist/portal` grep) (INV-4).
- *Given* the agent principal or a system principal, *when* it calls any search route, *then* it is refused: agents use their own registered read tools (C17), never the palette index.

### 3.3 Start from nothing: Recent and Create

**Story 3.1.** As Hannah, opening the palette with nothing typed shows where I was and what I can start.
- *Given* Hannah's recents at E0 (fixture: Northwind Robotics 401(k) Plan · PY2025 EBP Audit; 2025 Form 1065 · Ridgeline; Copperleaf Coffee Roasters, Inc. · Close · 2026-08 · "In progress" + "4 days past due"; Session · TY2025 season progress by office · "Needs your answer"; Agent · Season Progress Reporter · "Next run Mon, Sep 21 · 9:00 AM CT"), *when* she presses ⌘K, *then* SHL-02b shows Recent (5) and Create (7 actions in the R2 order).

### 4.3 Endpoints

| Method and path | Purpose | Matrix action | Errors (codes → words in `domain/search/messages.ts`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POST /v1/search` | Body `{q: str (1–100 chars after trim; control and bidi characters stripped), types?: [group], limit_per_group: 1–10 = 5, context?: {object_type, object_id}}` → `{groups: [{group, items: [{object_type, object_id, title, context, status: {code, context} \| null, due_on?, tag?, use_count?, target: {screen_id, params}, match_ranges: [[start, end]]}], has_more}], suggestion: {title, context, target} \| null, total_visible: int (≤ 99)}`. `types: ["agents"]` with an empty `q` lists installed agents (state c; served only when `search.agent_mode` is on and an `agent_definition` projection is registered). **There is no `hidden_by_wall` field.** Search text is never in the URL | `search.read` | 403 `forbidden` (role) · 403 `flag_off` · 422 `q_invalid` · 429 `rate_limited` (per-person token bucket, inferred: 10/s, burst 20) · 503 `search_unavailable` (statement timeout 1 s) |
| `GET /v1/search/recents?limit=5` | Up to 5 (max 10) recent items in the item shape above, re-filtered by `visible()` at read time | `search.read` | 403 `forbidden` · 403 `flag_off` |
| `POST /v1/search/recents` | `{object_type, object_id}` → 204. Resolves the document, runs `authorise()` with the type's read action; answers 404 and stores nothing if the viewer may not read it; upserts and trims to 20 | `search.recent.record` | 404 `not_found` · 403 `flag_off` |

All three routes are registered through `AbacusRouter` with exactly one action each (ADR-013, ROUTE-001). Every Pydantic field carries a classification tag (`backend/src/abacus/kernel/classification.py`): titles, context, related names and suggestions `confidential`; identifiers, codes, dates, counts and ranges `internal`.

**Query.** For each registered group in canonical order, a `LATERAL` subquery selects up to `limit_per_group + 1` candidates:
`tenant_id = current AND group_key = :g AND (tsv @@ websearch_to_tsquery('simple', :q) OR title % :q OR related_text % :q OR identifiers_text ILIKE :q || '%') AND <visible(ctx, read_action, scope_kind) on the document's columns>`, ordered by `ts_rank_cd + similarity(title, :q)` and then `source_updated_at DESC`, with `SET LOCAL pg_trgm.similarity_threshold = 0.3` and `SET LOCAL statement_timeout = '1s'`. One- and two-character queries use the prefix branch only. For read actions whose conditions `visible()` cannot express in SQL (consent, `mfa_recent`), the service post-filters each candidate with `authorise()` over a bounded candidate set (4 × limit). `data_class = tri` documents add the offshore-consent condition. `match_ranges` are computed server-side over the returned strings. The suggestion is the best trigram match over `title` and `related_text` **within the same visible predicates**, returned only when every group is empty.

## 6. Accrued Agent

**None.** P-02 makes **no model call** and adds no capability, tool, prompt or eval suite. It touches no `ai_gateway` or `agents` code, and an import-linter contract (`platform.search` must not import `abacus.ai_gateway` or `abacus.modules.agents`) plus a test keep it that way.

The palette's only agent-adjacent behaviour is navigation:
- Agent mode (`>`) lists agent definitions the viewer may start; ↵ opens WB-01 with the agent and context attached, and the **human** sends (INV-1; ADR-025: the agent acts within the invoking person's scope).
- A saved prompt loads into the composer, and nothing is sent.

Neither path creates an `agent_runs` row (T09 asserts this). The agent principal is denied `search.read`; agents read through their registered tools (C17). Chapter 10's `access_containment` eval and the walls suite are unaffected; T09 adds the palette's INV-3 cases to the walls test family.
