## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Let an audit team finish an engagement inside Accrued, from the engagement quality review to a sealed, immutable archive, with every judgment reserved to a named person and every step attributed:

1. **G6** EQR concurrence over a code-built digest of the partner's significant judgments (SQMS 2).
2. **Completion**: a checklist that names exactly what blocks the report, subsequent events through the report date, legal letters and the going-concern conclusion.
3. **G7** a representation letter composed from firm templates and the findings, approved by the EP, e-signed by the client and dated as of the report date (AU-C 580).
4. **Governance communications** (AU-C 260 planning and completion, AU-C 265/703) approved and sent by the EP to those charged with governance, with acknowledgements and the 60-day clock.
5. **The report**, assembled from structured choices and firm template blocks, never free-typed, approved by the EP as an L4 sign-off.
6. **G8 release**: one EP-only transaction that re-checks G5–G7 on the server, applies the firm signature, freezes the report, publishes the pack into the deliverables registry (C10) and starts the AU-C 230 and AU-C 265 clocks.
7. **G9 assembly and archive**: a 60-day countdown, a nine-item checklist, a binder index, a lock that seals the binder (Merkle root over every evidence hash, the row export, the audit hash-chain root and the run-record hashes) into a COMPLIANCE-mode manifest, database triggers that make the locked work item immutable, and an enumerated `archived_write` exception for EP-approved post-lock additions.

Chapter 01's scope note is binding: "EQR (G6), completion checklist, representation letter (G7 via envelopes), governance letters, report builder, release transaction (G8) into the deliverables registry, assembly and archive (G9: 60-day clock, sealed manifest in Object Lock COMPLIANCE mode, DB immutability triggers, enumerated archived_write exception, packaged run records). Schema migrated before Dec 10, 2027; code merges dark; flipped per firm in a Tuesday window before Dec-YE 2027 reports."

**M4 `NNNN_archived_guard` (T13, S29)**

- Function `archived_guard()` (plpgsql, `SECURITY DEFINER` owned by the migration role, `search_path` pinned): for the row's `work_item_id` (or `engagement_id` for engagement-scoped tables) it looks up `work_items.archive_locked_at` (or `engagements.status = 'archived'`) by primary key; if locked and `current_setting('app.archived_write', true)` is not a kind allowed for this table, it raises SQLSTATE `AB409` with message `archived`.
- Triggers `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OR DELETE … FOR EACH ROW` attached to every table that the module registries (F-03-T07 `TABLE_OWNERS`) mark as work-item- or engagement-scoped, including the review, workpaper, assurance, adjustments, requests, evidence-metadata and agents tables of earlier epics.
- `tenant_table()` attaches the guard automatically to any new table with a `work_item_id` or `engagement_id` column unless `kernel/db/archive_guard.py` lists it as exempt with a reason. Exempt by design: `audit_events`, `outbox`, `notifications`, `realtime_events`, `idempotency_keys`, `legal_holds`, `hold_objects`, `retention_policies` (they govern retention or delivery, not documentation content); allowed by kind: `archive_additions` and `archive_addition_decisions` (`archive_addition`), `post_issuance_events` (`post_issuance`, Q-05), the evidence retain-until column (`retention_extend`, extend-only CHECK), tombstone columns (`destruction_tombstone`, P-14, after retain-until).
- The GUC is set only by `uow.allow_archived_write(kind)` inside the four allowlisted service functions; banned pattern **ARCHIVE-001** in `backend/src/abacus_tools/quality/banned_patterns.py` names those files.
- The lock's own final unit of work writes `archives` and the G9 rows **before** it sets `work_items.archive_locked_at`, so the guard never blocks the lock itself.
