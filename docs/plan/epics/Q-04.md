## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** Turn "walkable in production" into "live at a real firm" safely, and keep it live through three busy seasons, with two founders and no year-one exception:
1. **A written, machine-checked freeze contract.** ADR P-29's windows (Jan 1 – Apr 20 for every live firm; Sep 1 – Oct 20 for firms live on tax or EBP work), mini-freezes, deadline weeks and the Tuesday 19:00 CT slot live in one protected file, `deploy/windows.yaml`, and the release pipeline, the flag CLI, the migration lint and the flag registry all read it (Q-04-T001…T005, T010).
2. **A release train that never deploys per fix.** Batched merges on the Tue/Thu 17:00 CT windows (F-01), staging once per batch, production weekly on Thursday from M9 and in the Tuesday 19:00 CT slot inside windows, a hotfix lane only for broken production, a security fix or an explicit request (chapter 01 §1.3.3, ADR P-56).
3. **Per-firm enablement as data.** Plans and modules are entitlements (ADR P-28), rollout is flags with expiries calendared outside every window (ADR-089), and every flip is a reviewed, audited ops request with a written rollback (§3.4).
4. **An onboarding kit that costs no deploy.** Each firm loads its own clients, PBC templates, organizers, email templates, branding and standards through admin screens, never through a commit (working agreement "content belongs in the database").
5. **Named people on call, a partner desk and a reserve that is measured.** N holds SEV1/2 with a named backup per window; AY is the partner-facing owner; the 10–30% support reserve is booked per sprint and compared with actual hours (RK-07).
6. **Pilot metrics computed by code**, weekly, per firm, as counts (W4–W7 and INV counters), so every gate (M10, M12, M14, each retro) reads the same numbers.
7. **A rollback that works on the day**: flags off for the firm, the A-01 binder export per engagement, and the firm's own tool authoritative until each engagement meets the system-of-record criteria.

## 3. Features & user stories

Invariants (chapter 01 §1.2): **INV-1** the agent never decides; **INV-2** citations verified; **INV-3** walls hold; **INV-4** portal scopes isolated; **INV-5** attribution correct. For an operations epic they constrain what operators may do: no agent changes its own stage or any flag (INV-1); metrics and dossiers carry code-computed counts, never model text (INV-2); walls exist before the first enrolled engagement and support sessions never cross them (INV-3); no real client is invited before M10 and metrics never leave a tenant except as counts under a pilot code (INV-4); every flip, release, enrolment and content load names the person who did it and, for flips and releases, the person who approved it (INV-5).

### 3.1 The freeze contract as code (A)

**Stories.**
- As N, I want one file that says which firm is in which window on which date, so the pipeline, the flag CLI and the migration lint agree without anyone remembering dates.
- As AY, I want the 2027–2029 calendar printed in one command, so window notices to firms and renewal trains come from the same source.

**Acceptance criteria.**

### 4.9 The window calendar, 2027–2029

Deadline dates follow the weekend and legal-holiday shift (IRC §7503); Emancipation Day (DC) moves Apr 15 when it is observed on the next business day. **[verify each year against C21's due-date reference set before the R4 review]**. A deadline week is the Monday–Friday week containing the shifted deadline. A mini-freeze runs from the shifted extension deadline minus 7 days through the deadline, 8 calendar days inclusive (this reproduces chapter 01's 2027 dates Sep 8–15, Sep 23–30 and Oct 8–15; OQ-1).

| Window | Applies to | Dates | Mini-freezes | Deadline weeks (no promotion, no flip) | In-window Tuesday 19:00 CT slots |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Autumn 2027 | Firms live on tax or EBP work | Wed Sep 1 – Wed Oct 20, 2027 | Sep 8–15, Sep 23–30, Oct 8–15 | Sep 13–17 (Wed Sep 15), Sep 27 – Oct 1 (Thu Sep 30), Oct 11–15 (Fri Oct 15) | **Sep 7, Sep 21, Oct 5, Oct 19** |
| Season 2028 | Every live firm | Sat Jan 1 – Thu Apr 20, 2028 | — | Mar 13–17 (Wed Mar 15), Apr 17–21 (Tue Apr 18: Apr 15 is a Saturday and Emancipation Day is observed Mon Apr 17) | Every Tuesday **except Mar 14 and Apr 18** (Jan 4 … Apr 11) |
| Autumn 2028 | Tax or EBP firms | Fri Sep 1 – Fri Oct 20, 2028 | Sep 8–15, Sep 25 – Oct 2, Oct 9–16 | Sep 11–15 (Fri Sep 15), Oct 2–6 (Sep 30 Sat → Mon Oct 2), Oct 16–20 (Oct 15 Sun → Mon Oct 16) | **Sep 5, Sep 19** only |

Outside windows: production promotes weekly on **Thursday** (chapter 01 §1.3.3; the time, 10:00 CT, is chapter 08's inference), and flips for firms outside their window may use any Tuesday or Thursday release day with the same approval and rollback fields.
