### 3.1 Programme spine and company foundation (A)

**A1 — One tracker, one vault (T01).** As both founders, we want every external clock in one place, so that no approval silently stalls.
- *Given* chapter 06 §7.6's vendor table, *when* T01 lands after F-01-T01 makes the repo private, *then* `docs/ops/vendors/README.md` *(new)* has one row per application with owner, start, typical lead time [verify], needed-by date, fallback and linked decision, and each row has a GitHub issue labelled `vendor`.
- *Given* any contract, quote, LOI, partner firm name or counsel memo, *then* it lives in the vault, and the tracker holds only status, dates and a vault reference. Counsel advice stays out of git to protect privilege; prices stay out because vendor terms are often confidential.
- *Given* the repo is still public on Mon Oct 19, *then* no issue is opened and nothing is committed until F-01-T01 is done.

**A2 — Company foundation before partner contracting (T02, RK-59).** *Given* Decision Day (Fri Oct 23), *when* both founders review the formation documents, founder agreement and IP assignments, *then* they are filed in the vault and noted beside DEC-03; *if* any is missing, partner contracting pauses until it is signed (chapter 11 RK-59).

**A3 — Spend under one envelope (T03, T59, T129, T207).** *Given* DEC-03 on Oct 23, *when* the envelope is approved, *then* the vault ledger carries each chapter 01 §1.6.7 line ($125k–265k in total, inferred), the cash position and months of runway; *and* every commitment needs both founders' signature (money never defaults on silence). *When* monthly spend exceeds the envelope pro rata for two months, or a quote is >25% above its line, *then* RK-05's action applies: optional vendors are deferred, gate spend (SOC 2, pen test, insurance, counsel) never is.
