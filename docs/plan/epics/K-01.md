# K-01 · Knowledge library & methodology v1

> Epic specification and task backlog. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for dates, owners and scope. Decision and ADR IDs are chapter 09's (DEC-nn, ADR P-nn). **AY** = ayushkbhatia, **N** = notanaskhan. The CSV twin of §9 is `sections/epics/K-01.tasks.csv`.
>
> **Short version.** K-01 makes the firm's methodology **database content with a lifecycle**, and gives it the three screens every later audit, engine and agent epic cites:
> 1. **KNW-01 Knowledge library**: one catalog (11 categories, a review queue, a change log) built over a provider slot, so later epics add their categories without touching it. It ships in MVP-1 with the PBC-template import that design partners use to onboard (chapter 01 §1.6.1).
> 2. **KNW-03 Program editor**: programs and steps with stable, version-pinned anchors, so a workpaper citation (SHL-07) lands on the exact step. Drafts with mandatory locks that only the QM owner changes.
> 3. **KNW-02 Methodology versions & import review**: approve a version with an effective date (MVP slice, S12). Later, the code-computed diff, the standards mapping and the **Methodology importer** (Accrued Agent) that parses a Word/PDF methodology into cited steps for a named human to approve (S22–S25, merged dark, flipped after the Oct 20 window).
>
> Nothing in K-01 lets the agent decide. Nothing in K-01 puts firm content into a commit.

## 1. Goal and user-visible outcome

**Goal.** The firm's methodology becomes versioned, governed **database content** that people approve and agents cite: a catalog of everything the firm has published, a program editor whose steps are the targets of workpaper citations, and a review-and-approve flow with effective dates. Content arrives through import and admin screens, never through a commit (§1.3.3; RK-63).

**What Brightwater Hale people can do when K-01 ships:**

- **By S13 (Fri Apr 30, 2027), in the M8 set:**
  - **Margaret Okonkwo** (Managing Partner, QM owner) opens **Knowledge** and sees in one place that *BH Audit Methodology · EBP v3* governs the 14 EBP audits and v2 is superseded, what waits for her approval, and what was approved and when (KNW-01 a–d).
  - She uploads a workbook of programs, steps and the 24-request PBC template; it lands as **Needs review**; she approves it with an effective date of Jan 1, 2026, and v2 becomes **Superseded** from Dec 31, 2025 (KNW-02 e, f). New EBP engagements accepted on or after the effective date get v3; the agent's attempt to approve is refused and audited.
  - **Ana Cruz** (staff) reads the published library; drafts and the + menu are hidden from her (KNW-01 e).
  - At a design-partner firm, the firm's QM partner does the same with the firm's own PBC template, so internal shadow (**PW1, Jun 1, 2027**) starts from the firm's own request list held in the database.
- **By S21 (Fri Aug 20, 2027), before the Sep 1 window:**
  - **Marcus Delaney** (senior) clicks ↗ in a citation on the Northwind program workpaper and lands on BH-EBP-110 step 4, lit, with its assertions, standard, PBC link, level and lock (KNW-03 a, b).
  - **Hannah Leclerc** (audit senior manager) creates *Draft v4*, edits optional steps, adds step 17 and submits it; mandatory steps stay locked to Margaret (KNW-03 c). Margaret sees the four v2 → v3 changes in BH-EBP-110 (KNW-03 e) and downloads the program to Excel.
- **By S25 (Fri Oct 15, 2027), dark; on per firm from Tue Oct 26, 2027:**
  - Margaret uploads *BH Audit Methodology — EBP v3.docx* (with its PDF print) and *BH EBP Programs v3.xlsx*. The **Methodology importer** (Accrued Agent) reads 96 pages and proposes 67 steps, each citing the page it came from, verified by code; three are flagged. She resolves the flags (step 7's range table split across pages 15–16), reviews the diff against v2 (4 added · 9 changed · 1 removed · 54 unchanged), maps the standards set (GAAS nonissuer · AU-C 703 · SQMS 1) and approves (KNW-02 a–f).
  - On real firm data, the importer runs only after its Bedrock evals pass, the firm opts in and the flag is flipped. Until then the workbook path covers every pilot firm.

**What K-01 does not do.** It does not let the agent approve, lock, unlock or publish anything; it does not update an engagement's pinned version (pin-once stays until K-03, ADR P-41); it does not send anything to clients; it does not store licensed publisher text; it does not touch tax return information.

**Obligations.**
- **Content in the database (§1.3.3; RK-63).** Pilot firms' PBC templates and programs are imported through KNW-01/KNW-02 or the import endpoints. The seed loader writes only the synthetic Brightwater Hale tenant. Standards sets are published reference rows (DEC-76).
- **Immutability and audit.** Published versions are immutable (trigger + insert-only identity table); every transition is a version event and an audit event; source files are write-once. These are SOC 2 change-management evidence: firm content changes without a deploy, with a named approver.
- **Retention (DEC-71, ADR P-14).** A version pinned by any engagement is part of that engagement's audit documentation and is retained at least as long ("Audit documentation 7 years, locked" in the seed, FIL-02). K-01 declares the retention record type `methodology_version` (clock starts at the last referencing engagement's archive date; reference-counted); P-14's engine enforces it. Nothing in K-01 deletes a version.
- **Licensed content (D8, ADR-056, DEC-19, DEC-94; RK-38).** Accrued supplies no publisher text. A firm's uploaded methodology is the firm's own content and responsibility. Card descriptions in the seed contain no licensed standards text; standards appear as references ("AU-C 315"), never quoted. The eval set includes a licensed-text detector case so the importer never adds text that is not in the source.
- **Scope exclusions.** Standards sets are GAAS nonissuer, SSARS and SQMS 1 only; no PCAOB issuer set (X-07) and no FFIEC/NYDFS/OFAC framework (X-01); a data test enforces both.
- **SQMS 1.** The importer is a technology resource. Its runs are recorded per version so the firm can evaluate it under its SQMS 1 monitoring; EBP v3 step 15 ("Technology resources: agent runs used and who adopted the output") is seeded content, not code.
- **§7216 / TRI.** Not applicable: methodology holds no tax return information. The PII-pattern hold stops a misfiled client document before any model call.
- **E-signature, connectors, client-facing text.** None. Connectors stay read-only; nothing reaches a client except PBC request wording, which P-06 sends only after a person publishes the binder.
