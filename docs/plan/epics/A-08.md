# A-08 · Audit fieldwork depth

*Epic specification and task backlog, written Sunday 2026-10-18 for Sprint 0 starting Monday 2026-10-19. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for order, dates, owners and scope. Chapter 09 is the register of record for `DEC-nn`, `DF-nn` and `P-nn` IDs; this spec never cites chapter 03's or chapter 06's own P-34+ numbers (ADR numbers in brackets are the F-02 reservations, e.g. P-09 = ADR-134). **N** = notanaskhan, **AY** = ayushkbhatia. The machine-readable backlog is `sections/epics/A-08.tasks.csv`; §9 mirrors it. Repo paths are relative to `github.com/notanaskhan/abacus` at `a42034c`. Paths marked \* do not exist yet (they are created by this epic or by an upstream epic named beside them); every other path was checked in the clone.*

> **Short version.** A-08 turns the audit cockpit from "evidence in, binder out" into real fieldwork for the 2028–29 season, on five P1 screens:
> 1. **Testing workspace (AUD-12)**: AU-C 240 journal-entry testing over a GL population proved complete by code, risk criteria and scores computed in code, a seeded selection, the agent testing each selected entry against its support with cited values, staff adopting at L1, exceptions to AUD-15, plus tests of details and controls on A-05's shared attribute grid, and the R2 "Expand testing" flow that only a named approver can release.
> 2. **Analytics (AUD-13)**: AU-C 520 flux with a dual threshold derived from performance materiality, explanations that count only with a verified source, and unexplained lines turned into firm-worded client questions (POR-05).
> 3. **Confirmations (AUD-14 + POR-17)**: an AU-C 505 control log where nothing is sent to an address the client alone supplied, the client signs only the authorization (C7), and respondents answer on a tokenised, firm-branded single page through a new **link principal** (ADR P-09) that the client never sees.
> 4. **SSARS workspace (AUD-26)**: review (AR-C 90), compilation (AR-C 80) and preparation (AR-C 70) on one cockpit, with expectations, inquiries and the "No assurance is provided" legend.
> 5. **Two red-zone extensions owned by N**: the protected GL model gains posting user, entry time, source and approver (ADR P-58), and the identity layer gains link principals with the `/v1/confirm/*` route family on the ROUTE-001 exception list.
>
> **The agent never decides** (INV-1): it proposes cited attribute values, explanations and reconciliations. People adopt, accept, approve expansions, send, and raise exceptions.
