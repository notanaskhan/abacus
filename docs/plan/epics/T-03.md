# T-03 · TY2027 season readiness: organizer editor, extensions & rules tables

> Epic specification and task backlog, written Sunday 2026-10-18 for the plan whose Sprint 0 starts Monday 2026-10-19. Chapter 01 is the plan of record for dates, owners and scope. Decision and ADR IDs are chapter 09's (DEC-nn, ADR P-nn); ADRs are cited by P-ID only, because F-02 reserves only ADR-116…130 and mints every later number at drafting in `docs/adr/backlog.md` (its erratum voids any other quoted number). P-68 is an F-02 proposal pending addition to chapter 09 §9.4.2, with DEC-77 as the record of decision. **AY** = ayushkbhatia, **N** = notanaskhan. The CSV twin of §9 is `sections/epics/T-03.tasks.csv`.
>
> **Short version.** T-03 makes the tax-pilot firms ready for the 2028 season (PW3, Jan 1 – Apr 20, 2028) before the freeze cut. It ships three things, all on the coordination layer that T-01 and T-02 built for the 2027 extension pilot:
> 1. **The organizer editor (BND-03).** Firm staff write organizer questions with exact client wording, conditional follow-ups and deterministic answer effects, on a binder or on a versioned organizer template. Published template versions are immutable and apply to the next season's binders.
> 2. **Extensions, estimates and payments (TAX-13).** A practice-level schedule of every extended return, with its safe-harbor check, payments, client confirmation and acknowledgment, plus every client's next estimated-tax installment. Code computes every figure. A named reviewer approves payment amounts. A person sends every client request. Accrued records filings and never transmits them.
> 3. **TY2027 rules tables.** Due dates with installments, 2028 holidays, the TY2027 8879 form set and tolerances, and thresholds, published as database content through K-02's two-person operator CLI by **Tue Dec 7, 2027**. No deploy is needed and no rule content goes into a commit.
>
> **Nothing in T-03 calls a model.** The Accrued Agent proposes nothing here, decides nothing and sends nothing (INV-1). The likely-to-extend signals, answer effects, safe-harbor targets and installment amounts are all code (ADR-050).

**Obligations.**
- **IRC §7216 / ADR P-12.**
  - Organizer answers, extension amounts, payments and estimates are TRI and tagged `tri`. Offshore users without consent never receive them (I-OFFSHORE).

They are encoded as reviewed parameters with golden tests and **CPA review recorded before the Dec 7 publish** ([verify] each season).
- **Due dates (IRC §7503).** Weekend and legal-holiday roll, including DC Emancipation Day, from the published holiday set. The tax CPA reviews the TY2027 golden dates.
- **Pub 1345 / DEC-85.** The TY2027 8879 form set and tolerances are re-verified by counsel. They never default.
- **Retention (DEC-71 / ADR P-14).** Extension records, filed forms and payment confirmations follow the tax-return record type's floors. T-03 adds no new retention class.
