# From completion to the locked archive

This is the order Accrued walks an audit from fieldwork's end to a locked file. Each step names
the screen, who acts and the gate it records. Code computes every gate; a person decides each one.
The Accrued Agent drafts and checks, but it never approves, signs, releases, sends or locks.

1. **Completion (AUD-19).** The team clears the completion checklist, subsequent events and legal
   letters. The engagement partner decides going concern on the decision card. The agent's
   recommendation is a suggestion, never the decision.
2. **Engagement quality review (AUD-18), G6.** The assigned EQR reviews the significant judgments,
   raises and clears notes and concurs. Only the assigned EQR can concur, and the EQR never signs
   the team's workpapers.
3. **Representation letter (AUD-21), G7.** The letter is drafted from the findings and the
   summary of uncorrected misstatements, approved by the partner and signed by the client. Its
   date must equal the report date.
4. **Governance communications (AUD-22).** The AU-C 260 planning letter goes before fieldwork. The
   completion letter goes before release, and the AU-C 265 and 703 letter within 60 days after.
   The partner approves and sends each letter; the client's committee acknowledges it.
5. **Report (AUD-23).** The report is built from the structured choices and approved by the
   partner. The agent never chooses the opinion or a modification.
6. **Release (AUD-24), G8.** With G5 to G7 met and every also-required item ✓, only the engagement
   partner releases, with a fresh second factor. Release delivers the pack and starts two clocks:
   the archive (AU-C 230) and the AU-C 265 letter, each due 60 days after the release date.
7. **Assembly and lock (AUD-25), G9.** The binder index is built by code, and the nine archive
   items are computed, never ticked by hand. At 9 of 9 a manager or the partner locks the
   archive with a fresh second factor. The contents become read-only and are sealed with a
   SHA-256 hash, and the manifest is kept in COMPLIANCE mode for at least five years.
8. **After the lock.** Nothing in the file changes. A later item is a post-lock addition: it needs
   a reason and the engagement partner's approval, and the original stays unchanged.

If a release or a lock is refused, the screen names the gate or item that blocks it. Fix that
item and try again; nothing is recorded on a refusal.
